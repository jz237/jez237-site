Start with your tank

  1. Set your working volume. Use “My tanks” or estimate it with Tank volume. Saved volume fills the appropriate tank-based calculators.
  2. Choose a question. Browse a category or search. Star the tools you use most often.
  3. Choose, slide, or type. Tap a common-size button when one matches your tank, bag, bottle, or equipment. Drag a slider for quick adjustments, or type an exact number. Slider ranges are editing shortcuts, not recommended limits. The picture and results update immediately. US and metric inputs describe the same quantities.
  4. Check the assumptions. Open “The math & sources” for formulas, limits, and references. Estimates are not measurements.
  5. Keep or share. Print the current result, copy a link with its inputs, or export your toolbox backup.

There is no universal fish-per-gallon rule, pH-mixing formula, medication dose, or guaranteed safe glass thickness. Those decisions need species, product, or engineering information beyond a calculator.
