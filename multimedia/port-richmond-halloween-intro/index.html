About this film

≈88 sReal-time WebGLThree.jsHalloween 2026Unreal Engine 5 game

HIDE is a real-time Three.js horror short set on PORT RICHMOND's real Witte St block on Halloween night, built from the game's own street kit, layout and audio. It is not a video: the street, the infected and every shot are drawn live, frame by frame, in your browser, and the sound is mixed as it plays.

  • One neighbour's last minutes on the first night: the block's Halloween display, a parked car, the gap under another car and a front room that should be safe. Framed wide shots cut with first-person hiding places, and three scares that each hit differently.
  • The title menu at the end is an HTML recreation for this page. The game itself runs in Unreal Engine 5, so the menu cannot start it here.
  • Skip (S or Esc) lands on the title screen at any moment; Replay (R) runs the film again. Weaker devices get lighter shadows, effects and resolution.
