Checkout & Online Orders

Browse products, build a cart, and finish checkout through The Hidden Reef's secure online store. Final discounts, shipping, tax, and availability are confirmed at checkout.

The Hidden Reef catalog helps you plan before you visit or order online. Product pages show current store information where available, but aquarium inventory can move quickly, especially livestock, frozen foods, and popular equipment.

For the most accurate answer on stock, pricing, pickup timing, or compatibility, contact the store or continue through checkout.

Good to know: account discounts, rewards, shipping, tax, and final totals are calculated during checkout after any required login.

Before You Checkout

Check the product details

Choose the exact size, color, model, or package before adding an item to your cart. Some products have multiple variants with different prices.

Helpful to review

  • Tank size and compatibility
  • Replacement part model numbers
  • Food size, package size, or formula

Ask the store about

  • Livestock compatibility
  • Water testing results
  • Equipment fit before a special trip

Cart & Pricing

Pickup, Shipping & Special Orders

Plan the easiest way to get what you need

Some items are easy to ship. Others are better handled in store, especially live animals, frozen food, heavy aquariums, rock, sand, and fragile glass.

Good online order candidates

  • Dry foods and additives
  • Replacement media and parts
  • Standard equipment and supplies

Best to confirm first

  • Live fish, coral, and plants
  • Large aquariums and stands
  • Special-order equipment
