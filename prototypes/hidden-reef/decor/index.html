Make decor useful

Hides, plants, and hardscape reduce stress when they give fish clear shelter and break up sightlines.

Design in layers

Foreground, midground, background, and one focal piece usually looks cleaner than filling every inch.

Keep maintenance possible

The layout should still let someone clean glass, vacuum waste, move water, and catch a fish if needed.
