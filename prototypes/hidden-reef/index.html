Website strategy

Keep the store personality, upgrade the buying path.

The current site already has the bones of a useful online store. The opportunity is presentation: stronger visual hierarchy, better category storytelling, and clearer reasons to buy from a trusted local aquarium shop instead of a generic marketplace.

  • Show real livestock and coral photography higher on the page.
  • Separate beginner, advanced, pond, and reef shopping paths.
  • Make shipping, pickup, and MAP-pricing notes readable.
  • Add educational content that supports product discovery.
