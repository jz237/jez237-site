Model notes & references

Original procedural reconstruction of the FiltoClear 5200, based on OASE product photos, exploded diagrams and operating instructions. Shapes, hidden channels and flow paths are simplified; proportions are illustrative. Selectable assemblies group repeated fasteners. This is an educational exhibit, not manufacturer CAD or a servicing procedure.

Before real service, follow the manual’s pump, pressure and electrical isolation instructions. Never operate an exposed UVC lamp.
