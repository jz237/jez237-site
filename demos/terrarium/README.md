# Weatherglass Rainforest Terrarium

A living rainforest under glass with moss, ferns, a waterfall, a pool of schooling neon tetras and a fire-bellied toad. Grab clouds to make rain, shake them for lightning, stir wind, pour fog, mist plants and wipe condensation. Tap the pool with Feed to feed the fish.

During a storm, the Tornado button (or T) brings a tornado down from the rotating cloud: a wall cloud lowers, a debris whirl spins up on the ground, and the condensation funnel reaches down to meet it. It wanders under its storm, leaning as its foot drags behind (grab the cloud to steer it), sucks up loose leaves, lashes the plants, wraps itself in rain and turns into a waterspout over the pool, then ropes out and lifts after a minute or so. Under a calm sky the button feeds the biggest cloud into a storm first. Press it again to send the tornado back up.

The bearded dragon now lives in the separate dry [Bearded Dragon Terrarium](../bearded-dragon-terrarium/). No dragon is loaded, rendered or simulated here.

- Public route: https://jez237.com/demos/terrarium/
- Source: scripts/terrarium/
- Build: npm ci && npm run build
- Tests: npm test
- Keys 1–7 select tools, T calls or calms a tornado, H hides controls, and ?ui=0 is a clean presentation view. ?tornado brews a storm and brings a tornado down on load.
