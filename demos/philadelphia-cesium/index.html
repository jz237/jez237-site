Make yourself at home.

Mouse: drag to move; wheel to zoom; middle-drag or Ctrl + drag to tilt and orbit.

Keyboard: Tab to the controls. Use + / − to zoom, H for home and Escape to stop orbiting.

Detail streams for the view in front of you. Pause after moving to let the finest available tiles arrive. Full detail is on by default.

Photographic 3D is captured imagery, not a live view. Coverage and sharpness vary by location. Place pins identify mapped locations.
