A MICROBIAL COMMUNITY ON EVERY GRAIN

The sand is a home.

A film of attached microorganisms develops on the wet grain. Flow transports dissolved compounds and oxygen across the water–biofilm boundary.

NH₄⁺AMMONIUM→NO₂⁻NITRITE→NO₃⁻NITRATE
AmmoniumNitriteNitrateOxygen

Two oxidation steps.

Ammonia-oxidizing and nitrite-oxidizing organisms perform aerobic nitrification. Oxygen is consumed and alkalinity is used up. The animation traces that sequence; it does not simulate chemical concentrations.

Mature is different from moving.

A new bed can fluidize immediately, but biological establishment takes time. Lower the establishment or oxygen control to see the illustrative activity decrease without assuming the water is safe.

Nitrate still needs a destination.

Nitrification changes nitrogen’s form. It does not remove it from the aquarium. Water changes, plant uptake or a separate denitrification process may manage nitrate.

The grain, biofilm, cells and molecules are enlarged independently for clarity. Cell colors identify teaching roles, not actual microbial colors. No species-level identification is implied.

OPERATION PAUSED / MAINTENANCE STUDY

Keep the bed balanced.

Before the biological stage

Remove suspended waste upstream. A fluidized biofilter is not a substitute for a mechanical solids filter.

Watch the bed height

Biofilm growth changes media behavior. Inspect freeboard, distribution and signs of carryover; use the manufacturer’s specified flow and expansion range.

Plan for a power interruption

A settled bed can impose extra resistance on restart. Keep protective non-return devices functional and follow the actual restart procedure. Avoid forcing an obstructed return.

Service deliberately

Isolate electrical power and follow the equipment manual before opening or disconnecting anything. Inspect the pump, feed path and distributor; avoid indiscriminate cleaning that removes established biofilm.

Measure the biology

Monitor ammonia, nitrite, oxygen, pH and alkalinity as appropriate to the system, especially during establishment and after interruptions. This exhibit is not a commissioning or sizing guide.
