THREE DISTINCT ROUTES

A screen is crossed
in two directions.

01 / Filtration

Dirty interior → screen → clean exterior → downstream treatment.

02 / Washing

Rinse pump → fixed nozzles → outside of screen → inward through mesh.

03 / Export

Loosened debris → internal trough → separate waste outlet.

Gravity-fed, specifically.

The upstream level is the reference. As screen resistance grows, downstream pumping draws the clean side lower. A pump-fed installation can instead build a higher upstream level; the two arrangements should not be confused.

Clear is not chemically clean.

A drum removes suspended material. Dissolved waste needs other treatment. The moving-bed study explains a biological stage that can follow.

Sources, model notes & limitations

An original gravity-fed teaching design. Drum geometry, exposed gear train, levels and accelerated cycle timings are illustrative, not a replica, installation plan or sizing tool. Half the drum is clipped in cutaway; the complete cylinder is restored in Assembled view.

Particles and screen openings are enlarged. The simulation tracks relative sector loading and a simplified head response; it does not calculate fluid dynamics, pressure, flexible debris capture, water consumption or chemistry. Fault behavior is demonstrative and varies on real equipment.
