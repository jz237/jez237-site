THE SURFACE IS THE HABITAT

Attached, not floating.

The enlarged study shows an irregular film attached to fibers. The shapes and colors explain a process; they are not microscopy images or literal depictions of species.

Oxygen reaches a moist film.

Microbes convert ammonia toward nitrate through aerobic nitrification. Oxygen and alkalinity are needed. Water carries dissolved substances to the attached community; air exposure helps renew oxygen at the wet surface. Cyan markers descend toward the film in the microscope.

Established takes time.

The New wheel experiment reveals fewer colonies and pale fibers. Moving the establishment slider compares conditions; it does not accelerate a real aquarium’s cycling. Use actual ammonia and nitrite measurements to assess a system.

Conversion is not export.

Nitrate remains dissolved after nitrification. Plant uptake, water changes or suitable separate treatment are needed to manage nitrogen accumulation. The wheel cannot replace every other part of aquarium care.

Three different processes.

The blue pad captures particles, activated carbon adsorbs selected dissolved compounds, and attached biology transforms nitrogen compounds. The model keeps those stages distinct.

Keep the living part.

A normally discolored biological wheel is not an exhausted cartridge. Marineland distinguishes routine cartridge service from care of the wheel and its axle.

Cartridge-change animation

  1. Disconnect power and lift the cover.
  2. Remove the loaded cartridge.
  3. Fit the appropriate prepared replacement.
  4. Seat the assembly and fill the box.
  5. Restart and observe the return.

Axle-care animation

The wheel lifts out intact and remains moist in a service tray while the bearing contact is inspected. The pleated surface is retained. Follow the actual manufacturer’s directions for cleaning and reassembly; this is a simplified animation of human service.

Before blaming the wheel

Check the water path, cartridge, intake, bearing seats and whether the assembly is seated correctly. Uneven rotation alone is not a reason to replace a biological wheel. Never run a real pump dry.

The manufacturer’s current manual governs installation and service. Use the model for understanding, not as a substitute for those instructions.
