About this model & credits

An independent visual study of human anatomy. Pieces are the named meshes of the open Z-Anatomy model, simplified for the web; small branches of vessels, nerves, ligaments, lymph nodes and skin regions are merged into their parent structure. It is a teaching model, not a clinical reference.

Model and descriptions: Z-Anatomy (Gauthier Kervyn and contributors; app by Lluís Vinent Juanico), CC BY-SA 4.0. The derived 3D assets and description text on this page are shared under the same licence.
