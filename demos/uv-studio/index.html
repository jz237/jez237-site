TREATMENT WITHOUT A TRAP

Damage to replication.

The enlarged cell remains intact. Amber bridges represent lesions in genetic material that may prevent replication. This is a conceptual animation, not microscopy or a prediction of survival.

UV intensity×Exposure time→Fluence

For uniform conditions, fluence is irradiance multiplied by time. Real chambers have unequal paths and intensities. Our three tracks explain the difference; they do not validate a treatment dose.

What changes exposure?

Lamp output, quartz cleanliness, water UV transmission and residence time all matter. Particles can shield microorganisms. A 36 W electrical rating alone says little about exposure received by a particular organism.

What UV leaves behind

UV does not capture solids or remove ammonia, nitrite or nitrate. It has no lasting disinfectant residual. Attached organisms, organisms on fish, and water that has not passed through the unit can remain outside its reach.

Clarification is not a universal cure

Susceptibility varies among organisms. Controlling free-floating algae is different from treating a specific pathogen. Do not infer disease eradication from a clear aquarium or a glowing lamp.

HUMAN SERVICE / SIMPLIFIED SEQUENCE

Keep the barrier intact.

  1. Disconnect power and isolate water; let the unit cool.
  2. Open the cap and withdraw the lamp straight.
  3. Align the replacement lamp’s pins with its socket.
  4. Inspect the quartz and seals; clean as directed.
  5. Reassemble without forcing the glass.
  6. Restore water, check for leaks, then restore UV power.

The animation combines lamp replacement and sleeve inspection into one teaching sequence. It is not a repair manual. Use the current instructions for the exact unit and replacement parts.

A lit lamp can still be due for service

UV output declines with use. Follow the specified replacement schedule, rather than judging output by visible light. Sleeve deposits require separate attention.

Keep UV enclosed

Never inspect an energized lamp outside its protective housing. UV can harm eyes and skin. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for dry electrical connections, drip loops and GFCI protection.
