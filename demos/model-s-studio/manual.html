01 / INTERACTIVE OPERATING GUIDE

Using the studio.

See every part together

Choose All parts on screen. All 308 selectable pieces receive individual, non-overlapping spaces. The board includes 297 artist surfaces and 11 added schematic battery pieces. Each piece is scaled independently for visibility; this layout is not to a common engineering scale. Use Fit every part to restore the complete arrangement after zooming.

Explore the assembled car

Drag with one finger or the left mouse button to orbit. Use the wheel or a two-finger pinch to zoom. Use right-drag to pan. Perspective, Side, Front and Top provide quick camera positions. Auto orbit starts a slow rotation only when you request it.

Explode and reassemble

Move the explosion slider from 0 to 100, or press Explode model. Panels, glazing, cabin surfaces and wheels separate in space; the added battery moves out from beneath the floor. This spatial view can contain overlap from some angles. The all-parts board provides the unobstructed alternative. Reassemble restores the original placements.

Inspect a piece

Click a surface or select it from the searchable component list. The inspector displays its visual category, approximate model bounds and triangle count. Isolate piece removes surrounding geometry and moves in for a close-up. On the board, isolation gives that piece its own larger space. Clear restores the current system view.

Look inside the battery

Choose Battery anatomy for a close-up of the added pack layers. The tray, cooling plate, five illustrative cell banks, rail, cover, connector and electronics housing are separately selectable. In Visible system, choose Battery to filter either layout to these eleven pieces.

Reset, playback and accessibility

E toggles explosion; R resets assembly, camera and filters; Escape clears selection. Paint is retained on reset. Play sequence runs an explode, hold and reassemble cycle. Reduced-motion preferences disable transition easing. On phones, zoom the parts board or use fullscreen when individual pieces are small.

04 / THE ADDED BATTERY ASSEMBLY

The floor has a story, too.

The original downloadable car asset contains no high-voltage battery. This studio adds an underfloor pack so the exploded view can explain where stored energy lives in an electric vehicle. The extra pieces are always identified as schematic in the inspector.

This is an educational reconstruction, not Tesla CAD. The five drawn cell banks, their visible cell caps, dimensions, cooling plate and interconnection rail are illustrative. They do not establish the real module count, capacity, cell count, circuit design or service procedure. Do not use the animation as instructions for opening or servicing a high-voltage pack.

Enclosure and lid

A lower tray and a sealed cover enclose the illustrated pack. Their broad, flat shape places the assembly underneath the cabin. In the spatial exploded view, the pack moves beside the car to remain visible above the presentation platform.

Thermal layer

A blue-gray plate represents heat transfer to a liquid cooling circuit. Tesla confirms liquid cooling; this drawing does not reproduce the production channel paths.

Cell banks and connections

Five illustrative banks contain repeated cylinder caps to make the packaging legible. The connection rail, orange connector and electronics housing show general functions while avoiding a claim of exact electrical architecture.

What the numbers mean

The studio now has 308 selectable pieces: 297 reconstructed artist surfaces plus 11 schematic battery pieces. The count is a count of visual groups. The all-parts board scales them independently; inspector dimensions describe their modeled bounds only.

Context: Tesla — Electric Vehicle Components and 2021+ Model S high-voltage battery assembly documentation.

05 / THE VISUAL MODEL

Materials, detail and provenance.

The model uses metallic silver clearcoat, patterned rubber, fine upholstery grain, carbon-style weave, brushed metal and tinted glazing. A purpose-built softbox environment provides continuous reflections across the body. Geometry simplification has been disabled to retain the available source detail. The lighting and finishes are visual interpretations.
