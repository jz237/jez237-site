About this model & credits

An independent visual study of the Tesla Model S Plaid. Pieces are artist-authored surfaces, not a verified service-parts catalog. Hidden powertrain and battery internals are not modeled.

Model: Tesla Model S Plaid 2021 by 3D Hawk, CC BY 4.0. Adapted for component separation, optimized geometry and interactive paint.
