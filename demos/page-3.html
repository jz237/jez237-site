Jurassic Terrarium

A miniature prehistoric world under glass, sculpted from research and rendered in Blender. A lipped Tyrannosaurus prowls a valley of tree ferns, cycads and horsetails, hunting and retiring to its rock den at night. A Triceratops herd may stand its ground against it, Stegosaurus graze and flee, a Brachiosaurus browses the treetops and Pteranodons soar under the clouds. Grab the clouds to make rain, stir the wind and run the sun and seasons.

  • Research-based dinosaurs sculpted & baked in Blender
  • Walk, sniff, roar, drink & sleep in a den
  • Click an animal for its field-guide card
  • Follow-cam for close-ups

Betta Bowl · Living Silk

A betta lives in a glass bowl of real simulated water, with driftwood, anubias, a floating almond leaf and its own bubble nest. Feed it: the glass lid lifts off, pellets drop onto the water and the betta rises to take them from the surface. Tilt the bowl and the gravel slides, the leaf rides the water and a hard shake breaks the nest, which the betta rebuilds.

  • Feed it pellets through the lid
  • Drag to tilt the bowl, its water & gravel
  • Bubble nest it breaks & rebuilds
  • Flare fins, air gulps & mirror displays

Living Silk · Betta Atelier

Look closely at a halfmoon Betta in turquoise and crimson. Orbit its iridescent scales, follow the rippling fins, and explore the tiny details of its eyes, gills and branching fin rays. An intimate living study built with six GPT-generated reference images, including magnified head, scale and membrane studies.

  • Magnified head, scale & fin textures
