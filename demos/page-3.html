Mechanical Flower: Exploded View

A jeweled-enamel clockwork flower that assembles and comes apart on a 28-second loop. Watch twelve enamel petals, the stamen cage and glowing core, the filigree gear ring, drive gears, braided stem, retention collars and leaf panels fly out to match a ten-callout engineering infographic, then close back into a blooming flower on a dark studio backdrop. Drag the Explode and Bloom sliders to scrub it by hand.

  • 28-second explode and bloom cycle
  • Ten labelled parts, 84 floating gold screws
  • Scrub Explode and Bloom by hand
  • Poster and studio looks
  • Flashing jewel glints and drifting sparks

Jurassic Terrarium

A miniature prehistoric world under glass, sculpted from research and rendered in Blender. A lipped Tyrannosaurus prowls a valley of tree ferns, cycads and horsetails, hunting and retiring to its rock den at night. A Triceratops herd may stand its ground against it, Stegosaurus graze and flee, a Brachiosaurus browses the treetops and Pteranodons soar under the clouds. Grab the clouds to make rain, stir the wind and run the sun and seasons.

  • Research-based dinosaurs sculpted & baked in Blender
  • Walk, sniff, roar, drink & sleep in a den
  • Click an animal for its field-guide card
  • Follow-cam for close-ups

Betta Bowl · Living Silk

A betta lives in a glass bowl of real simulated water on a studio table: a gnarled driftwood arch, dark river rocks and plants that sway in the current, in 3D on a bed of sand, lit by the lamp in its lid. Feed it: the lid lifts off, pellets drop onto the water and the betta rises to take them. Tip the bowl and it rolls on the table while the water and sand slosh round the rocks, or wait for it to rest, flare at its reflection and rebuild its bubble nest.

  • Feed it pellets through the lid
  • Tip it to roll it; its water & sand respond
  • Plants that sway with the water
  • Flare fins, air gulps & mirror displays
