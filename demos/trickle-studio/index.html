Model notes & manufacturer sources

An original wet/dry teaching design informed by manufacturer filtration and modular-sump instructions. It is not a replica, measured CAD, plumbing plan or maintenance guide for a particular product. No tank capacity or flow rating is implied.

Ribbed bio-balls, tray perforations, plumbing, rotor and baffles are modeled in 3D. The main sump surface uses planar reflection and depth-guarded screen refraction. Other water surfaces use simpler shaded waves. Water gathers at outlet rims, follows rib arcs, and detaches into drops. Streams deflect around approximate media envelopes. The acrylic has thickness, polished edges and subtle surface marks. The overflow study sections the drain to reveal entrained air; gradual pad loading and staged restart are qualitative demonstrations. Impact crests, floor-light patterns, wet sheen, bubble removal, loading and drain-down remain illustrative; there is no fluid or chemistry solver.
