Explode a complete human body into 2,270 named anatomical pieces across ten systems, then open seventeen nested exploded views, from the heart, lungs, spine and brain to the skull, eye, hand, knee, rib cage, digestive tract, urinary system, face, aorta, pelvis, shoulder, larynx and foot.
