Mechanical Flower: Exploded View

A jeweled-enamel clockwork flower that assembles and comes apart on a 28-second loop. Watch twelve enamel petals, the stamen cage and glowing core, the filigree gear ring, drive gears, braided stem, retention collars and leaf panels fly out to match a ten-callout engineering infographic, then close back into a blooming flower on a dark studio backdrop. Drag the Explode and Bloom sliders to scrub it by hand.

  • 28-second explode and bloom cycle
  • Ten labelled parts, 84 floating gold screws
  • Scrub Explode and Bloom by hand
  • Poster and studio looks
  • Flashing jewel glints and drifting sparks
