Map key & data notes

Markers show the latest available NHC map advisory. Colored points show forecast intensity; the slider steps through official forecast positions. A cone may end before five days if the forecast is shorter.

D · DepressionS · Tropical storm1 · Hurricane23 · Major45Other / post-tropical

Development areas show the chance of a tropical cyclone forming within seven days, not the chance of a hurricane hitting a location. Coastal lines show hurricane warnings (red), hurricane watches (pink), tropical storm warnings (blue), and tropical storm watches (yellow). Inland hazards and storm surge are not fully represented here.

Map layers come from the NOAA tropical weather service and are checked every ten minutes while this tracker is visible. Map publication may lag NHC advisories. Post-tropical systems without an NHC forecast may no longer appear. Wind speeds are converted from knots; times use your device’s time zone. Use the latest NHC advisories and local emergency guidance.
