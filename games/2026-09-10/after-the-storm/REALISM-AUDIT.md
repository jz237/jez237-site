# Six-part graphics and physics audit

| Requested improvement | Implemented evidence | Verification evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hull forces | `hydrodynamics.js`: twelve contact patches, individual immersion/relative-speed pressure and planing lift, moments/inertia, intake wetness and slam drag. `race-core.js`: yaw inertia/contact authority. | `realism.test.mjs`, `hydrodynamics.test.mjs`, `water-response.test.mjs`; all course/class routes and championships pass. |
| Real-time physics and speed feel | `race-view.js`: normal playback rate 1, fixed 1/60 steps, speed-dependent 58–76 degree field of view and lower chase view for both players. | Visible real-time verification: 6.733 game seconds over 6.980 wall seconds including startup; actual speed benchmark and airborne camera inspection. |
| Sharper crests and breaking foam | `wave-model.js`: horizontal trochoidal displacement, CPU inverse queries, shared GLSL spectrum/Jacobian. `foam-field.js`: retained foam, advection, decay and shallow-breaker sources. | CPU/rendered-vertex inversion error tests; inspected storm crests and foam through a jump and full race, then reduced excessive coverage. |
| Spray and persistent wakes | `wake-field.js`: world-space wave packets sampled by physics and renderer. `spray-sheets.js`, `hull-spray.js`, `spray-physics.js`: contact-loaded chine sheets, ballistic droplets, wind-dragged mist and attachment to the pitched/rolled hull. | Wake crossing, decay, mist wind response and spray attachment tests; rendered landing and four-rider race. |
| Rider suspension | `rider-pose.js`: fixed-length two-link IK, planted feet, rotating grip targets, upper-body reach correction and head counter-motion. `hydrodynamics.js`: damped compression from measured load. | Rider connectivity/length tests over speed, turn and compression; browser apex and 0.130 m landing-compression inspection. |
| Lighting and coast | `water-detail.js`: crest forward scattering and reflection edge fallback. `race-view.js`: weather-updated environment lighting. `land-materials.js`: wet rock color/roughness. `coastal-scenery.js`: terrain-conforming contact shadows. `foam-field.js`: shoreline breakers. | High-quality storm jump/race and Low-quality Amber split-screen rendering, no runtime errors; wet coast and vegetation shadows visually inspected. |

See `VALIDATION.md` for measured results and explicit approximation limits. High/Medium/Low control foam, reflection and screen resolution; normal gameplay and visible verification use the same physics inputs. The verification driver never teleports or grants progress.
