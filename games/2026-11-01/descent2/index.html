Playing in the browser

Click the game: the mouse flies the ship
Esc releases the mouse / game menu
⛶ button (top right) for full screen
F1 in-game help
Controls: Options → Controls
Gamepad: press any button to connect
Pad: left stick moves, right stick aims
Pad: triggers fire, bumpers bank, A/B up/down
Touch: left thumb flies, drag on the right to turn
Touch: KEYS opens the phone keyboard for names

Saved pilots and games are kept in this browser. Enhancements: native-resolution rendering with trilinear filtering and MSAA, high-resolution cutscenes with an edge-aware sharp upscale, smooth-edged text and HUD, glowing halos on the lamps, smoke trails behind missiles, smoking damaged robots and sparks where shots hit and robots blow up, HDR rendering with glow and a filmic tone curve, per-pixel lighting from shots, flares, explosions and the headlight with relief and shine on the textures and robots, ambient occlusion, light haze and a visible headlight beam, flowing lava, rippling water and shimmering force fields, explosions with layered fireballs, shockwaves, glowing debris and scorch marks, reflections on smooth metal and water, a test set of extra-sharp textures (Level 1, upscaled with Real-ESRGAN) and a new detailed Medium Lifter robot (shape made with Tencent Hunyuan3D-2, powered by Tencent Hunyuan; add ?newship to the address to meet one at the start), smooth display-synced frame rate, the original CD soundtrack, the game's own MIDI music played through a General MIDI synthesizer, or a music folder of your own (title, briefing, credits and level tracks; a playlist.txt sets the level order). From D2X-XL: four-times-sharper textures, matched to each level's colors, and detailed 3D models of the robots (moving as the originals do), the ship, missiles and power-ups. New pilots get mouse and gamepad flight set up; adjust them in Options → Controls. On phones and tablets, on-screen controls appear.
