what am I watching?

an ecosystem with no scripted behavior. everything you see emerges from a few simple rules, running live.

plankton — drift and bloom where the light falls. food for grazers.
grazers — school together as real boids, flee predators, graze the bloom, and breed.
predators — hunt the grazers.

every creature carries genes — speed, size, senses, fear, colour — that mutate a little each time it breeds. the ones that survive pass theirs on, so the population evolves in front of you: watch the grazers grow faster over generations, because the slow ones keep getting eaten.

because predators eat grazers and grazers eat plankton, their numbers rise and fall in linked waves — press S to see the population oscilloscope, G for the live gene-drift chart. if everything dies out, a genesis event reseeds the world. it runs forever.
