# First Light: Keystone Waters — concept

## Elevator pitch
A first-person angling sim on real Pennsylvania water where the lake itself is the interface. You read wind lanes, shade lines and nervous water on a hyper-real surface, put on polarized lenses to sight-fish through it, and every fish you hook is a photo-real, persistent individual that remembers your lures. Dawn on Lake Nockamixon, in real time, with the real weather.

## Pillars
1. **The water is the interface.** No sonar icons by default. Baitfish dimples, a swirl behind a laydown, a foam line on a point, the dark band of a weedline, a carp's bubble trail: the renderer is the HUD.
2. **Every fish is somebody.** A finite population of individually simulated, photo-real fish that hold on structure, feed on light and pressure, follow lures, learn from being caught and grow over seasons, with a few named legends per lake. The direct answer to fishing games that spawn fish on cast at fixed spots.
3. **Real place, real time.** Lake Nockamixon (Bucks County, 1,450 acres, 20 hp limit, Big Bass Program regulations; musky and tiger musky, walleye, largemouth, smallmouth, pickerel, hybrid stripers, channel cats, carp, panfish). Real sunrise, real weather feed, real seasons.
4. **The catch is a moment.** The underwater strike replay, the in-hand hero render with wet skin and fin translucency, the measured photo on the board, the catch card, the release that kicks the ripple field.

## Borrowed from Fishing Planet, and what we leave behind
Adopted: the balanced-tackle chain (weakest link fails first, hook size gates bites); two-sided fight tension (slack throws hooks, overload breaks gear, side pressure turns fish) rendered diegetically with optional pro meters; a named retrieve vocabulary with a technique recognizer; size classes (Young / Common / Trophy / Legend); species cards with a bite clock filled from your own catches; accelerated time with a free skip; float, bottom and trolling techniques alongside casting; line abrasion with a free retie; an optional late-unlock sonar; a guided first morning.

Left behind: licenses, travel fees, premium currency, cooldowns, repair bills, XP-gated tackle (ours unlocks from catches), fish spawned on cast, equipment-ratio difficulty, repeating audio, deep menus. Twelve species on one real lake done to the bone rather than 143 across 19 maps.

## Modes
Free Fish (4× time by default, free skip, real-time toggle with live weather), First Light sessions (20-minute dawn or dusk targets on a daily seed; Night Cats and Walleye Troll variants), Big Bass (kayak catch-photo-release format, best five by length, against AI kayak anglers), **Watch Demo** (a smart self-playing episode: the AI guide fishes the real lake with legible decisions, filmed by an event-driven director with coverage accounting; take the rod at any moment), Lure Cam / Aquarium, Species Gallery, Photo Mode, First Morning with Ray.

## Progression
No RPG trees. Species and size-class milestones unlock lure families, rig presets and one rod/reel/line option per tier, each a real trade-off. Journal completion by species and class, the lake-knowledge map, daily challenges, Stories.
