HOW TO PLAY

THE LANCE

It fires on its own. Hold to charge through three tiers and release. The third is a screen-long beam that kicks back and overheats: the Lance locks until it cools.

THE ANCHOR

Shoot a supply drone to collect one. It cannot be destroyed, it blocks bullets and it hurts whatever it touches. Docked to the nose or tail it fires the core weapon that way.

Press Anchor to throw it. It stays out, joined to you by a live tether that cuts anything it sweeps through. Press again to call it home; touch it with your nose or tail to dock it there. While it is docked, hold back and press Anchor to swap it to the tail, or hold forward to bring it back to the nose.

CORES

Arc chains lightning. Ricochet bounces off walls. Burrow crawls along them. The same core again raises its level.

CONTROLS

Arrows/WASD move   Space Lance   X Anchor
Q/E speed down/up   Esc pause

Controller: stick, A Lance, X Anchor, bumpers for speed.
Touch: drag anywhere to move, hold CHARGE, tap ANCHOR.

SURVIVING

One hit kills. You restart at the last checkpoint with a level-one Anchor. Every enemy appears the same way every time, so each death teaches the route.

Kills made with the tether or the Anchor build a chain up to x8.
