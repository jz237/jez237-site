# Ravine 1800

Original Quarry Impact vehicle artwork, authored in 2026. No third-party vehicle model, marque, badge or texture is incorporated.

The open tubular chassis, roll cage, molded nose and side pods, bucket seats, exposed air-cooled rear flat-four engine, front fuel tank, double-wishbone presentation and all-terrain wheels are generated by `src/buggy-asset.ts` and `src/buggy-geometry.ts`.

Artwork, collision deformation and sound remain beta. The 760 kg mass, rear-wheel drive and 2.40 m wheelbase describe an original game vehicle, not a validated replica of a real buggy. Visible suspension fabrication is authored for presentation; gameplay uses the shared vehicle suspension model. Existing bundled four-cylinder sound is reused rather than a dedicated flat-four recording.
