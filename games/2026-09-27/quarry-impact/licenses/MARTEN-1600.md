# Marten 1600

Original Quarry Impact vehicle artwork, authored in 2026. No third-party vehicle model, marque, badge or texture is incorporated.

Bodywork, glazing, rear flat-four engine, front luggage bay and trim are generated by src/marten-asset.ts. The original Rook wheel tooling and Tern cabin tooling are reused without changing those vehicles. Existing bundled four-cylinder sound is reused with a 0.94 pitch multiplier; it is not a dedicated flat-four recording.

Vehicle artwork, severe-crash details and sound remain beta. Rear-biased mass and rear-wheel drive are gameplay approximations, not a validated replica of a real vehicle.
