# Rook 1100

Original Quarry Impact compact saloon artwork, authored in `src/compact-asset.ts`. It uses no donor vehicle meshes, external textures, manufacturer marks, or reference-game assets. The GLB includes the original closed body pressings, interior, inline-four engine bay and stamped wheels.

Gameplay uses Quarry Impact’s shared suspension, damage, paint, glass and audio systems. The compact reuses the existing four-cylinder audio bank; it has no separately recorded engine soundtrack.
