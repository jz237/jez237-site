The flock comes in at dusk. Hold the lighthouse. Call its beam down when they stoop.

HOW TO PLAY

Machine-birds arrive in ribbons, wheel overhead as a flock, then settle into formation and stoop on you. One hit costs a ship.

THE BEAM

The lighthouse sweeps the sky on its own. Call the beam and it drops a column of light on you for a few seconds: anything inside is dazzled, slowed, silenced and worth double. It takes ten seconds to recharge.

SCORING

Birds shot mid-stoop are worth double. Quick kills build a chain up to x5. Catch a falling ember for fifteen seconds of twin shot. Down a red Kite and its escorts lose their nerve.

Every third wave is a passing flight that does not shoot back: clear all forty for a bonus. Every fifth is the Roost.

CONTROLS

Left/Right or A/D move   Space fire   X beam   Esc pause
Controller: stick, A fire, X beam. Touch: drag to move, tap BEAM.
