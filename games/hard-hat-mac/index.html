    HOW TO PLAY

    The job

    Eight shifts, one job each: set and rivet girders, round up lunchboxes, load crates, run the pumps before the pit floods, bridge a river, unbolt and scrap old beams, light storm beacons, and top out the tower. Falls of more than one floor, enemies and hazards cost a hard hat (life). Checkpoint flags save your spot.

    Medals & best times

    Every shift has three stars: ⏱ beat the par time, ❤ no injuries, ★ every bonus item. 1 star = bronze, 2 = silver, 3 = gold. Your fastest run comes back as a see-through ghost to race (switch it off in Settings), and top-10 times go on that shift's board. Each finished job joins the city skyline.

    Crew & trouble

    Vandal — pulls out girders you haven't riveted.
    Inspector — his hard hat makes him stomp-proof.
    Yard dog — fast, but can't climb ladders.
    Knock them out: swing your hammer (F / Y / BONK) or jump on the vandal and the dog. Out cold for a few seconds — chain knockouts for bigger points.
    Carrying something? The same button gives a shoulder shove that knocks trouble back without dropping your load.

    Power-ups

    ☕ Coffee: speed · ⛑ Gold hat: one free hit · ⏱ Stopwatch: freezes trouble · 💰 Cash: bonus points

    Keyboard

    WASD/Arrows move & climb · Space jump · E/Shift pick up, place, hold to rivet · F hammer bonk · Q/R turn camera · drag or right-drag mouse to look · wheel zoom · C recenter · Esc pause · M mute. Change keys in Settings → Keyboard controls.

    Gamepad

    Left stick move · Right stick camera · A jump · X/B action · Y bonk · LB/RB turn camera · Start pause

    Touch

    Left thumb: drag anywhere to move. Right side: JUMP, ACT and BONK buttons, drag empty space to look.

    Demo

    Watch Demo on the title screen lets the crew's best hand play all eight shifts, with a few camera cuts along the way. The demo also starts by itself if the title sits idle. Press any key to take over.

    Online co-op & assists

    Online Co-op: one player hosts a room and shares its 5-letter code, the other joins. Score and hard hats are shared. Assists in Settings slow the enemies, add time or give unlimited hard hats (assisted runs don't go on the boards). Voice & narration in Settings switches the crew's spoken lines off.

    ONLINE CO-OP

    Two Macs, one shift. One of you hosts a room and reads out its code, the other joins with it. Score and hard hats are shared.
