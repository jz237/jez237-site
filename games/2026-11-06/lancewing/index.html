HOW TO PLAY

You ride a skyray, an armoured winged mount, round a ring of floating ledges above a storm sea. Beat your wings to climb, steer with momentum, and duel: when two riders meet, the higher lance wins. Lances level? They clang and you both bounce apart.

An unhorsed rider drops an ember core. Grab it for chained points (250, 500, 750, 1000, and +500 for a mid-air catch) before it re-ignites into a stronger rider. Touching the sea costs a life.

DANGERS

Glowing rings on the sea mark spouts; red bands from the sky mark the spire's lightning; cracked ledges crumble. Stay too long in a wave and a Stormhunter comes: only a head-on lance into its eye brings it down.

RIDERS

Skirmishers wander, Lancers hunt and climb, Reavers dive (and feint), Shieldbearers block from the front, Twinblades come in pairs, Stormcallers cast lightning along a marked line.

BOSSES

Every boss has glowing weak points: strike them from above. Every attack is marked before it lands.

DIFFICULTY

Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare each have their own world boards. Harder levels score more (×0.6, ×1, ×1.5, ×2.2). Medals count on Normal and harder.

KEYBOARD

STEER: A D or ← → · FLAP: W, ↑, SPACE (tap fast to climb, hold for a steady beat) · PAUSE: Esc or P. LOCAL CO-OP: rider 1 A D + W, rider 2 ← → + ↑ or Enter.

GAMEPAD

STEER: left stick or d-pad · FLAP: A, X, RT or RB · PAUSE: Start. Two pads in local co-op: one each.

TOUCH

Drag left or right anywhere on the left side to steer; hold the FLAP button on the right.
