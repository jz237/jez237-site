HOW TO PLAY

You are an infiltrator working through a sealed research facility, one room at a time. Every room's walls are electrified: touch one and you're done, and so is any robot that does. The door you came in by seals behind you. Find the lift marked on your map; a boss guards it in every sector.

Security robots power up a moment after you walk in. A red glint on the floor means one is about to fire down that line. Robots die on the walls, when they collide, and in each other's fire: luring them is half the game. Destroy every robot in a room for a room-clear bonus.

Don't linger. After a while the SEEKER arrives: an orb nothing can stop that drifts through walls and comes for you, faster every time it returns. Move on.

POWER-UPS

» RAPID FIRE twice the fire rate⁂ SPREAD three bolts in a fan ⟲ RICOCHET shots bounce off walls⬡ OVERSHIELD soaks one hit, walls too ✺ EMP CELL +1 EMP charge

An EMP wipes out robots and bolts around you and staggers a boss. Between sectors, spend salvage on a better carbine, armour plates, dash jets, EMP charges and a Seeker jammer.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

RUN: arrow keys or W A S D · FIRE: hold SPACE to plant your feet and fire the way you point (the direction keys aim) · TWIN-STICK: run with W A S D and aim + fire with the arrows · MOUSE: aim at the cursor, left button fires · EMP: E, Q or the right button · DASH: SHIFT · PAUSE: Esc or P

GAMEPAD

RUN: left stick · AIM + FIRE: right stick, or hold A to plant and aim with the left stick · RT fires the way you face · EMP: X or B · DASH: a bumper · PAUSE: Start

TOUCH

Left thumb runs, right thumb aims and fires; EMP and DASH sit above your right thumb.

DIFFICULTY

Normal is a real fight from the first room and builds up room by room; Easy starts gently. Each level has its own world high-score board (×0.6, ×1, ×1.5, ×2.2 points). Medals count on Normal and harder.
