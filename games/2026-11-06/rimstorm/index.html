HOW TO PLAY

You pilot the RIMRUNNER, a hover-sled locked to the near rim of a lane-web that runs away into the depths. Enemies emerge at the far end and climb toward you. Slide from lane to lane and fire down your lane. Clear the web and you DIVE down the tube to the next one: crystal spines left in the lanes will wreck you, so shoot them away or slide out of their lane.

ENEMIES

SPLITTER climbs, then splits into two hoppers near the rimHOPPER hops lane to lane; at the rim it hunts you, and when your lane flares red (amber with colour-safe telegraphs) it pounces SPINEWRIGHT lays a crystal spine up its laneARC MITE crackles along lane borders: only hittable crossing a lane LANCER charges, then fires slow bolts up its laneSHOCKER lights a lane, then electrifies it MIRROR sends your first shot back up the lane

PURGE AND POWER-UPS

Each web you get one full PURGE (wipes the web) and a weaker one (clears the rim). Pods float up their lane: be in that lane when one reaches the rim. TWIN SHOT (double damage, hits mites on the borders), RAPID FIRE, a GUARD DROID that holds a lane, and HOP charges that lift you clear of grabs, bolts and strikes.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

SLIDE: A D or ← → (tap for one lane, hold to keep going), or move the mouse like a spinner (click the game to lock the pointer) · FIRE: Space, Z or the left button · PURGE: X, Shift or the right button · HOP: W, ↑ or the middle button · PAUSE: Esc or P

GAMEPAD

SLIDE: left stick (gentle tilt = fine control) or d-pad · FIRE: A or RT · PURGE: B or LB · HOP: X, Y or RB · PAUSE: Start

TOUCH

Drag a thumb left and right anywhere on the left of the screen to slide along the rim; it fires while your thumb is down. PURGE and HOP sit on the right. Works upright or sideways.

DIFFICULTY

Normal starts at a medium pace and ramps up web by web. Easy is a gentle start. Each level has its own world boards; harder levels score more (×0.6, ×1, ×1.5, ×2.2). A checkpoint every web: if you fall, you can carry on from the web you reached.
