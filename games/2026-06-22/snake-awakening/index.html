What Is
This?

Snake Awakening is a meta Snake game where the snake slowly realizes it is inside a game, then starts breaking out of the normal Snake rules. Basically: it is Snake becoming self-aware, hijacking its own genre, and trying to escape the premise.

It moves through meta arcade, RTS command mode, cozy farming, bullet hell, and a final rule-breaking escape. The snake can even play itself; touch, swipe, or press a key to take control whenever you want.
