HOW TO PLAY

Machines pour into the arena through the gates. Run one way and shoot another, scrap every machine to clear the wave, and bring in the survivors (green beacons): each one you reach in a row is worth more. A PULSE scraps small machines and bolts all around you; you get a few every wave.

POWER-UPS

⁂ TRIPLE BEAM three full beams» OVERCLOCK double fire rate ⟿ RAIL LANCE pierces, hits for two➶ SEEKERS shots curve onto machines ⟡ WINGMAN a drone fights beside you⧗ TIME WARP machines and bolts slow down ⬡ BARRIER +1 shield✺ PULSE CELL +1 PULSE

Machines sometimes drop a pod when scrapped (big ones more often), and a supply pod lands once every wave. Pods only wait about 13 seconds.

DIFFICULTY

Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare each have their own high-score tables. Harder levels score more points (×0.6, ×1, ×1.5, ×2.2). Medals count on Normal and harder.

KEYBOARD + MOUSE

RUN: W A S D · AIM + FIRE: arrow keys, or aim with the mouse and hold the left button · PULSE: Space, Shift or the right mouse button · PAUSE: Esc or P

GAMEPAD

RUN: left stick · AIM + FIRE: right stick (RT fires the last way you aimed) · PULSE: A or a bumper · PAUSE: Start

TOUCH

Left thumb runs, right thumb aims and fires, the PULSE button sits above your right thumb.

TIPS

Nothing arrives without a warning: gates glow red before machines step out, spitters show a pink aim line, pylons crackle before an arc, and every boss attack is marked on the floor first. Haulers crush survivors, so get people out of their way. If you go down you come back on the spot and the nearest machines are thrown clear.
