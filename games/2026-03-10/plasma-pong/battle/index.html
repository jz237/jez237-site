HOW TO BATTLE

Each side guards a reactor core behind a wall of seven shield cells. A ball that gets past your paddle cracks a cell; a ball that finds a gap hits your core. Destroy your rival's core to win.

MOVES

  • Aim — where the ball meets your paddle sets its angle. Edges send it wide.
  • Smash — move into the ball as it arrives (your paddle can lunge forward) for a faster shot.
  • Curve — sweep sideways as you hit: the ball kicks out, then bends back.
  • Plasma bolts — fire to stun the rival paddle, chip their shield, nudge a ball or snipe power-ups to claim them. Three charges; returns recharge them faster.
  • Overdrive — fills as you play (faster when you're hurt). Wider paddle, burning shots, unlimited bolts.

PILOTS

Beat a rival in the campaign to unlock them as your own pilot. Each one flies with a perk.

VERSUS, ONLINE & REPLAYS

  • Versus — a friend on the same screen or a CPU rival, with optional party twists (twin balls, tiny paddles, power surges and more).
  • Online — quick match, or create a room and send a friend the code or invite link. Best of three.
  • Instant replay — every winning hit replays in slow motion. Any key skips it.
