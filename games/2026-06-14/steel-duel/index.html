STEEL DUEL

HD remaster of Atari TANK (1974) · 2-player maze & minefield

CPU difficulty
First tank-combat arcade game, Kee Games 1974. Drive your foe into the mines.

HOW TO PLAY

Outscore your rival before the clock hits zero

PLAYER 1

W forward · S reverse
A/D steer · Space fire

PLAYER 2 / CPU

↑ forward · ↓ reverse
←/→ steer · Enter fire

Shells are stopped by walls. A hit (or a mine!) scores a point for your opponent, then both tanks respawn. Mines never come back — bait your rival into them. Press P to pause, C for 1974 mode, M to mute.
