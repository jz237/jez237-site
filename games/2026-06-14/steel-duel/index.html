Campaign (1P or 2-player co-op): survive waves of enemy tanks across 12 levels and defeat three bosses. Enemy types differ — scouts are fast, brutes are tanky, snipers keep their distance, mine-layers drop mines, and wardens (and the Mauler boss) have a front armor plate, so hit them from the side or behind. You have shared lives; clear a level to earn one back. Co-op revives a downed teammate next wave.

Versus: 2-player duel and survival co-op use the controls above. Online rooms use the room code, mode, and name fields; ready up, then P1 starts. Shells travel half the arena width, stop at walls, and chip walls open. Mines kill instantly and never come back. Campaign and Versus have separate leaderboards. P pause · C 1974 mode · M mute.
