Creature Arena

Rebuilt in the 3D particle style of the cat demo. You pilot a living point cloud through an arena of shock drones. Hits physically blast body particles away, and your abilities degrade until the cloud reassembles.

3D particle bodyEvery dot has a home position, velocity, and spring. Damage is visible as real displacement.
Regions matterLoose legs slow movement. Loose eyes shrink pickup vision. Loose core weakens your pulse.
ControlsWASD/arrows move. Drag or touch the field to orbit. Space or Pulse emits a gravity blast.
