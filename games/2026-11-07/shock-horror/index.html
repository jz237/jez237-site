HOW TO PLAY

On the 3rd of October 1911 the night shift went down the pit at Hallow Seam. Twelve men. None came up. You are going down after the gold they died for. Every level is a maze of tunnels: find the lift down at the bottom to go deeper. Keep your lamp lit. Do not answer the whispers.

THE SCARES

The pit is full of them: over three hundred different scares, from figures at the edge of your light to writing on the walls, the dead, the things that crawl, and the ones that leap out at you. Every new one you witness goes in the Fear Ledger. They cannot hurt you. The creatures can.

YOUR KIT

PICK breaks loose rubble, chips gold out of the veins and digs remains out of the walls (hold ↑ or ↓ to swing up or down). DYNAMITE blows open cracked boulders and sealed vaults (hold ↓ to drop it at your feet), so stand well back. FLARES blind shriekers and burn the Pale One away.

YOUR LAMP

The lamp burns oil. Pick up oil cans to keep it lit. With no oil the dark hurts you, and the Pale One comes for you. Light a fire to rest once (full hearts and lamp) and save your place.

FALLS

Short drops are fine. Long falls hurt, very long falls kill, unless you land in water. Ladders catch you.

DISCOVERIES

Every level hides a cursed relic in a sealed vault, and many hide remains in the rock, a horror to stumble upon and a page of the night shift's diary. They go in your journal. The map (M) shows what your lamp has lit. Most levels also hide a treasure map: find the red X and dig down (↓ + PICK).

THE LOCKER

Finishing collections earns lamp colours, pick skins and small perks (equip up to two).

KEYBOARD

MOVE A D / ← → · CLIMB / AIM W S / ↑ ↓ · JUMP SPACE (or K) · PICK J (or X) · DYNAMITE L (or C) · FLARE F (or V) · MAP M · PAUSE ESC

GAMEPAD

Left stick moves, climbs and aims · A jump · X or RB pick · B or RT dynamite · Y or LB flare · VIEW map · START pause

TOUCH

Drag on the left half to move and climb; the buttons on the right jump, swing, throw dynamite and flares.

CONTENT

Sudden loud jump scares, disturbing images, gore and flashing lights. Set SCARES to MILD and FLASHES to REDUCED in the options if you need to.
