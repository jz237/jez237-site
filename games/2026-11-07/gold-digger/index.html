HOW TO PLAY

Ride the old lift down into the mountain. Every level is a maze of caverns: find the lift down at the bottom to go deeper. Gold, gems and secrets are everywhere, and the further you look the more you find.

YOUR KIT

PICK breaks loose rubble, chips gold out of glinting veins and digs fossils out of the walls (hold ↑ or ↓ to swing up or down). DYNAMITE blows open cracked boulders and sealed vaults (hold ↓ to drop it at your feet) — stand well back! FLARES light up the dark, scare bats away and melt pale shades.

YOUR LAMP

The lamp burns oil. Pick up oil cans to keep it lit; with no oil the dark hurts you and shades come for you. Light a campfire to rest, refill the lamp and save your place.

FALLS

Short drops are fine. Long falls hurt, very long falls kill — unless you land in water. Ladders catch you.

DISCOVERIES

Every level hides a relic in a sealed vault, and many hide a fossil in the rock, a wonder to stumble upon and one of the old prospector's notes. They all go in your journal, and the more you find the higher your rank. The map (M) shows what your lamp has lit.

KEYBOARD

MOVE A D / ← → · CLIMB / AIM W S / ↑ ↓ · JUMP SPACE (or K) · PICK J (or X) · DYNAMITE L (or C) · FLARE F (or V) · MAP M · PAUSE ESC

GAMEPAD

Left stick moves, climbs and aims · A jump · X or RB pick · B or RT dynamite · Y or LB flare · VIEW map · START pause

TOUCH

Drag on the left half to move and climb; the buttons on the right jump, swing, throw dynamite and flares.
