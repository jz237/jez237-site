How To Play

Trap territory by cutting the field into pieces the Qix can't reach. Fill enough of the board to clear the level.

Move & draw: travel the glowing border safely, then cut inward into open space to draw a line (Stix). Close it back onto a wall to claim everything the Qix is sealed out of.
Slow vs fast: hold Shift (or the SLOW button / gamepad X) while cutting to draw slowly — claims made slow score 2×. Fast cutting is safer but worth less.
The Qix: the churning energy line in the open field. If it touches your live cut, you lose a life. On later levels a second Qix appears — seal a cut that splits the two apart for a huge bonus.
Sparx: sparks patrol the walls and edges of claimed ground. Don't let them reach you.
The Fuse: if you stop while cutting, a fuse ignites at the start of your line and races toward you. Keep moving to stay ahead of it.
Controls: Arrow keys / WASD, gamepad d-pad/stick, or touch (swipe, or on-screen pad). Enter/Start begins, P or Esc pauses.
