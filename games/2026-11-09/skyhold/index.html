HOW TO PLAY

You fly the WARDEN gunship round and round a small planet (it wraps: fly on and you come back round, through day and night). Settlers live in domed colonies on the ground. LIFTERS warp in, go down for a settler and carry it up: one that reaches the top of the sky fuses with its captive into a fast, vicious HUSK. Shoot the lifter and the settler falls: catch it and fly low to set it down. A settler dropped from low down lands safely by itself.

Clear every machine to end a wave. After four waves a CARRIER arrives: shoot its glowing weak points (line up with them; the hull stops other shots). Lose every settler and the colony falls: lifters turn into husks for the rest of that world.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

The line under the top bar always says what to do next. The SCANNER strip at the top shows the whole planet: settlers (green), lifters (amber, red when they have someone), everything else, and the bracket where you are. A red marker at the screen edge points to a settler being lifted.

WARNINGS

Nothing hits you without a warning: machines shimmer as they warp in, and glow before they fire. A carrier shows a line or a ring before each attack. Its hull is solid but safe to brush, except when it charges.

CHAINS, ENRAGED CARRIERS

Destroy machines in quick succession for a CHAIN: 4 in a row doubles their points, 8 triples them, 12 quadruples them (the meter under your nova count shows it running down). Once half a carrier's weak points are gone it is ENRAGED and attacks faster.

ONLINE RIVALS

Pick RIVALS on the online screen: two pilots on one planet for three waves, each with their own score. Save settlers, shoot machines, and shoot your rival while they carry a settler to knock it loose and steal the catch. Being shot down costs points; most points wins.

ONLINE CO-OP

Two gunships guard one colony and share the ships. Quick match pairs you with anyone looking, or host a room and share its 4-letter code. The host picks the world.

NOVA AND BLINK

NOVA destroys every machine on screen (and hurts a carrier's weak points); you carry a few. BLINK jumps you far round the planet to escape; it needs a few seconds to recharge.

DIFFICULTY

Easy, Normal, Hard and Nightmare each have their own high-score tables. Harder levels score more points (×0.6, ×1, ×1.5, ×2.2). Medals count on Normal and harder, and unlock new ship colours in Options.

KEYBOARD

FLY: A / D or ← / → (the ship turns to face the way you push) · CLIMB / DIVE: W / S or ↑ / ↓ · FIRE: Space or J · NOVA: Shift or K · BLINK: H or L · CAMERA: C · PAUSE: Esc or P

THE MACHINES

LIFTER comes down for your settlers and carries them up; at the top it fuses with its captive into a HUSK, fast and vicious (when a colony falls, every lifter becomes one). SOWER drifts high and sows floating mines. HIVE POD breaks apart as you hit it and splits open into a swarm of STINGERS. PROWLER comes for a pilot who takes too long over a wave. SKIMMER flocks are harmless points. Later worlds add a second wave: WHIRLER rings that spin around you and dive together, the COIL, a slow generator that keeps letting out SPARKS (destroy it first), the SCORCHER, which hops about and throws firebrands that curve after you, and the MAW, which opens its jaws and dashes in to bite. The first time you meet each one, a card names it.

THE SKYGATE

A great ring hangs in the sky on the far side of the planet (a ring on the scanner). Fly through it and it throws you straight to wherever a settler needs you most: a lifter carrying someone off, a settler falling, or a lifter going down. It needs a few seconds to recharge.

POD INTERSECTION

Some waves send two shielded hive pods closing on one point (a warning says so). When they meet they lock together for about three seconds, circling, linked by a crackling bridge: hit either one and both burst together for a 2,000 bonus, their stingers scattering away from you. Miss the moment and they drift apart and hunt you as usual.

DYNAMIC MUSIC

Each world's music has two more parts that join in as the fight heats up: drums when machines close in, then a pulsing lead when it gets fierce (a carrier, an event, HYPER). Options → DYNAMIC MUSIC turns them off.

CHASE CAMERA

Options → CAMERA → CHASE (or C in flight) puts the camera low behind your gunship, looking ahead along the curve of the planet. RIGHT (or forward on the stick) flies on the way you face and LEFT turns you about; the camera swings round with you. Markers at the bottom of the screen show machines and settlers behind you.

GAMEPAD

FLY: left stick or d-pad · FIRE: A or the right trigger · NOVA: B or the left trigger · BLINK: Y or a bumper · PAUSE: Start

TOUCH

Hold the phone sideways. Your left thumb flies (push left or right to fly that way, up or down to climb or dive); FIRE, NOVA and BLINK sit under your right thumb. Aim assist and AUTO-FIRE (the beam fires by itself whenever something is lined up) are on by default for touch; both are in Options.
