	*The* six level Descent][ anarchy series

		  by NuKE and Karash

ELEGY pl. elegies n.  "A poem which lamments the death of someone"

Discription:  "The Elegies" is a six level compilation that incorporates 
some of the best features found in almost all popular levels.  Built 
for their flexablity, almost all playing styles can find a home in at 
least one of these levels.  They also are very lag free...although 
computer lag is dependent on your hardware, "The Elegies" are optimized 
to increase average frames per second (at least on Karash's p90 ;-).  
"The Elegies" concentrate on small to medium sized levels.  NuKE's levels 
have a "in your face" theme with fast action and quick kills.  Karash's 
levels are bigger in size and give a more 3D dogfighting approach towards 
the level set.  The midi music also has been redone with some awesome 
tracks that we have scrounged up, and even includes a track by Metallica
and Nirvana....only for AWE32 users tho :-)

(And yes...Karash made "Speed Racer Pro", and NuKE is of "Kaotix" fame...)

Level 1:  "Hydrocide"
Discription:  Sleek fast level.  Be on your toes always in this one ;-)
Weapons:  3 Gauss (31,250 extra rounds) // 5 Super Lasers -
        with 4 Quads // 2 Plasma Cannons // 4 Homing Missles
        8 Mercury Missles // 2 Smart Missiles // 1 Smart Mine
        3 Afterburners // 2 Ammo Racks // 1 E.S. Converter
        2 Flags...(or 2 sheild powerups in anarchy play)

	16 Mercury Missles // 5 Smart Missles // 8 Guided Missles
