	First of all, yes, these are all Quake2 textures. This was a
pain in the ass considering I wasn't able to find a way to extract
the Quake2 textures directly. I was forced to take screenshots in Quake2
and cut out the textures manually in a paint program. The end result
doesn't look too bad and the level plays very fast.

Programs used: DMB2, DTX2, Paint Shop Pro 4.14

Time spent: 9 beers

Supported game modes: Anarchy, Flag, Hoard
