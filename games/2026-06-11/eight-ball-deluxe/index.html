House rules

Rack 'em up

Sink the rack. Knock down the 7 pool-ball drop targets, then shoot the lone 8-BALL target and the saucer behind it to clear the rack — your bonus carries the whole game.

DELUXE. Downed targets expose the D-E-L-U-X-E standups behind the bank. Spell it for a replay — and listen for the knocker.

Multipliers. The 4 in-line drops on the left (backhand them from the upper flipper) push your bonus to 5×. The left orbit is the Bank Shot — it climbs 10K→70K.

A-B-C-D lanes spot you a pool ball. Tilt warnings are real. The machine will talk trash.
