August 10, 2026

Habanero peppers turn red

The habanero has gone into full production, carrying an exceptional number of green peppers while the earliest fruit turns bright red.

Season notes
The plant is still flowering and setting more fruit, so harvest can stay selective: pick the fully colored peppers as needed and let the firm green ones keep sizing and ripening.
August 10, 2026

Jalapeños keep coming

A second August view catches the jalapeño still loaded with full-sized dark-green fruit, plus flowers and younger peppers for the next round.

Season notes
Foliage and fruit look clean and well supported. Regular picking now will keep the plant focused on the many flowers and smaller fruit still queued behind this harvest.
July 18, 2026

Jalapeños reach harvest size

The plant is carrying several glossy dark-green peppers at full harvest size, with flowers and smaller fruit still forming above them.

Season notes
Growth is upright and vigorous, the fruit surfaces are clean, and the canopy is still actively flowering. One yellowing leaf low on the plant is normal older growth; the rest of the foliage looks healthy.
