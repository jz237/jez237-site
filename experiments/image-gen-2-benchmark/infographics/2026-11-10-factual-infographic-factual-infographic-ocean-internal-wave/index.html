Ocean Internal Waves

Tidal generation and propagation of ocean internal waves

Research behind the infographic

  • Internal waves are vertical disturbances within ocean density layers. [1]
  • Tidal flow over a submarine ridge can generate internal waves. [1] [2]
  • Internal waves propagate through the ocean and can contribute to mixing water masses. [1]
  • Internal waves can produce surface patterns detectable in satellite imagery. [2]
