Atychiphobia — Intense Fear of Failure

An artwork-led explanation of intense fear of failure, possible triggers and symptoms, avoidance, and professional support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Atychiphobia means intense fear of failure; this is an established descriptive phobia name, not a separate diagnostic code. [1]
  • Potential failure in exams or job interviews can trigger fear; worry about failure can arise before the event. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms can include severe worry, palpitations, trembling and feeling sick. These are possible symptoms, not universal. [2]
  • People may avoid situations with potential failure; avoidance can impair work or school performance. [1]
  • A phobia can feel genuinely threatening; intense fear need not match the situation. A possible unsuccessful outcome is not the same as immediate physical danger. [1]
  • Atychiphobia and perfectionism are not the same; fear of failure differs from striving to be flawless. [1]
  • Talking therapy such as cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) can help phobias; seek professional help when symptoms affect life. [2]
