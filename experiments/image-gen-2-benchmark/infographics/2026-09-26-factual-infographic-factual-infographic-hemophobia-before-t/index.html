Hemophobia — Before the Blood Test

An evidence-based infographic explaining hemophobia in a supervised blood-test setting, possible anxiety symptoms, avoidance, risk perception, and professional CBT/exposure support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Fear of blood is a recognized example of a specific phobia. [1] [2]
  • A phobia involves intense fear or anxiety out of proportion to actual danger. [2]
  • Seeing or anticipating the feared object can trigger severe worry or immediate anxiety. [1] [2]
  • A phobia can cause dizziness, noticeable heartbeat, chest tightness, nausea, or trembling; responses vary. [1]
  • People may actively avoid a feared object or situation; avoidance can interfere with daily life. [1] [2]
  • Professional support can include cognitive behavioral therapy and exposure therapy. [1] [2]
  • Exposure therapy helps people engage with situations they have been avoiding and is a CBT method; treatment should be planned with a qualified professional. [2]
  • Someone whose phobia affects their life can seek help from a GP or other health care provider. [1] [2]
