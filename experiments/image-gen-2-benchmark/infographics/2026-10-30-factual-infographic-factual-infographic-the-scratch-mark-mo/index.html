The Scratch Mark — More Than Sharp Claws

How normal feline scratching combines visible marks, paw scent deposition, superficial sheath removal and stretching.

Research behind the infographic

  • Scratching is normal feline behavior, not evidence that a cat is seeking revenge. [1] [2]
  • Scratching leaves visible marks and deposits scent from glands on the paws, serving visual and olfactory communication. The scent is not visible. [1] [2]
  • Scratching helps remove superficial claw sheaths; the entire claw is not shed. [1] [2]
  • Scratching also stretches the body and maintains claw motion used in hunting and climbing. [2]
  • Cats differ in preferred scratching materials and in vertical or horizontal orientation. [1]
