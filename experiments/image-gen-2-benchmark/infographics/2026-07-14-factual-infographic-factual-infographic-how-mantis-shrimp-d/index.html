How Mantis Shrimp Detect Polarized Light

A museum-grade marine sensory-biology infographic showing how specialized stomatopod compound eyes detect linear polarization and, in some groups, circular polarization, with anatomy, optics, habitat context, and species-variation caveats.

Research added after publication

  • Experiments showed that studied mantis shrimp could detect circular as well as linear polarization. This is not the same as color vision, and the finding should not be generalized to all species without further evidence. [1]
