The Third Eyelid — A Protective Fold

Normal feline third-eyelid location, protective extension and contribution to the tear film.

Research behind the infographic

  • The nictitating membrane is the third eyelid: a whitish-pink membrane beneath the other eyelids at the inner corner near the nose. [1] [2]
  • The third eyelid extends across the eye to help protect the eyeball from scratches. Depict extension schematically, not a timed voluntary wink. [1]
  • Glands within the third eyelid contribute to the watery portion of tears. [1]
  • Upper and lower eyelids blink and spread tears over the eye surface, keeping it moist and clearing small particles. [1] [2]
  • The cornea is the clear dome at the front of the eye; the iris is the colored area surrounding the pupil. [1]
  • Seek veterinary care for apparent eye discomfort and discharge. [2]
