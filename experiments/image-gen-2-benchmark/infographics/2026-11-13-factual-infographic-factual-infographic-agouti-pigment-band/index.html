Agouti — Pigment Bands Within a Single Hair

Feline agouti pigment switching, hair-level bands versus coat-level stripes, and recessive non-agouti inheritance.

Research behind the infographic

  • ASIP interacts with MC1R to switch eumelanin and phaeomelanin deposition during hair growth, creating bands. [1]
  • In a brown tabby, hairs between dark stripes are banded; stripe hairs remain dark. Banding within a hair is distinct from the spatial tabby pattern. [1]
  • A/A and A/a support agouti banding; two recessive a copies produce non-agouti solid hairs. [1] [2]
  • Overall coat appearance depends on other genes; orange cats can retain tabby markings regardless of agouti genotype. [1]
