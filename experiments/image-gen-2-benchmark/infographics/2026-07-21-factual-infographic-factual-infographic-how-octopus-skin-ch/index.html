How Octopus Skin Changes Color and Texture

A real-world marine-biology bench infographic showing how octopuses coordinate pigment organs, reflective cells, and muscular skin texture for rapid camouflage.

Research added after publication

  • Pigment-filled chromatophores can expand and contract, altering which colors are visible at the skin. Rapidly changing the extent of these pigment cells helps an octopus adapt its pattern to its surroundings. [1]
