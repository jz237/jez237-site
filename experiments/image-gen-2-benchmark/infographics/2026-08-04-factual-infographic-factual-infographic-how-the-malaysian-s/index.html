How the Malaysian Stalk-Eyed Fly Turns Eyespan Into a Signal

A direct-canvas, insect-first scientific infographic showing how both sexes carry lateral eyestalks, why males have a more exaggerated relative eyespan, and how researchers connect that ornament with mate choice, ritualized contests, resource ownership, development, and reproductive quality.

Research added after publication

  • The study linked contest outcome to both male eyespan and whether the fly already held the resource. Eye span is therefore not the only factor to consider when interpreting a contest. [1]
