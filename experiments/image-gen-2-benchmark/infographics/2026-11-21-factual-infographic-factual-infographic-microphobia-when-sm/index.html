Microphobia — When Small Things Feel Overwhelming

Microphobia, its subjective threat response and evidence-based clinical help, depicted with an imagined small-object trigger.

Research behind the infographic

  • Microphobia means extreme fear of small things; it is described as a specific phobia, not a separately named diagnostic category. [1]
  • Thoughts of little things can provoke severe anxiety; fear can be out of proportion to actual danger, and most small everyday things cause no harm. [1]
  • Phobias can involve avoidance that interferes with everyday life. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms can include palpitations, trembling, dizziness and severe anxiety. [2]
  • Talking therapies including cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) can help manage phobia symptoms; consult a healthcare provider when fear affects daily life. [1] [2]
