How Fog-Basking Beetles Collect Water

A non-fiction visual explanation of how posture, fog, surface texture, droplet adhesion, coalescence, and gravity can work together in fog-basking beetles.

Research added after publication

  • Fog-basking is documented in particular Namib beetles, including Onymacris unguicularis. Their behavior helps them collect fog droplets; it should not be treated as a universal trait of all darkling beetles. [1]
