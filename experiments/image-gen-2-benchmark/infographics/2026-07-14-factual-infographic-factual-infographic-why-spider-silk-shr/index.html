Why Spider Silk Shrinks When Humid

A real-world biomaterials infographic explaining why many orb-weaver dragline silks shorten and tighten when exposed to high humidity.

Research added after publication

  • Humidity changes the mechanical behavior of dragline silk. Wet dragline fibers can supercontract, shortening when free to move and developing contraction stress when restrained. [1]
