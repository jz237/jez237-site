Why Spider Silk Shrinks When Humid

A real-world biomaterials infographic explaining why many orb-weaver dragline silks shorten and tighten when exposed to high humidity.

Archive source notes

Conservative synthesis of established spider-silk research: absorbed water alters hydrogen-bonded and other molecular interactions in dragline silk, permitting protein chains to rearrange and the fiber to contract; magnitude varies among species and silk types.
