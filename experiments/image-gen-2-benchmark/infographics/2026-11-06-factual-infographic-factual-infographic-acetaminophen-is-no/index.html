Acetaminophen Is Not Cat Medicine

Feline acetaminophen toxicity, blood oxygen transport, household exposure prevention and urgent veterinary contact.

Research behind the infographic

  • Acetaminophen (Tylenol) must never be given to cats; deficient liver breakdown makes it potentially fatal. [1] [2]
  • Acetaminophen can damage red blood cells and reduce their oxygen-carrying ability, and can damage the liver. [1]
  • Signs can include weakness, rapid or difficult breathing, blue-purple mucous membranes, vomiting, and facial or paw swelling. [2]
  • Acetaminophen may be present in multi-symptom cold and allergy products; keep medicines out of animal reach. [2]
  • Suspected ingestion requires immediate veterinary or animal poison-control contact; report accidental ingestion immediately. [2]
  • ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center hotline: (888) 426-4435. [2]
