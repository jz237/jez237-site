Ombrophobia — Extreme Fear of Rain

Source-backed definition, rain triggers, body alarm, avoidance and CBT help.

Research behind the infographic

  • Ombrophobia is an extreme fear of rain; this established descriptive name falls within specific phobia, an anxiety disorder. [1]
  • Triggers may include light drizzle, heavy rain or anticipating rainfall; worrying about forecasts and refusing to go outdoors can disrupt everyday activities. [1]
  • A person may recognize that their fear is unreasonable yet find it difficult to control; feared consequences are not evidence of actual danger. [1]
  • Phobias can involve severe worry, palpitations, trembling and dizziness; symptoms vary between people. [2]
  • Avoiding outings or skipping activities when rain is possible can restrict daily life. [1]
  • Talking therapies including cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) can help phobias; seek professional help when symptoms affect life. [2]
