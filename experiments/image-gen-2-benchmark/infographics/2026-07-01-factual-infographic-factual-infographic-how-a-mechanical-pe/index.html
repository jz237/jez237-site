How a Mechanical Pencil Clutch Advances Lead

A real-world engineering infographic explaining how a push-button mechanical pencil opens its clutch, advances graphite lead, and grips it again.

Research added after publication

  • The patent shows a lead-gripping collet controlled by a tapered receiver. Moving the pieces together and then returning the receiver separately advances the lead by a small step instead of letting it slide freely. [1]
