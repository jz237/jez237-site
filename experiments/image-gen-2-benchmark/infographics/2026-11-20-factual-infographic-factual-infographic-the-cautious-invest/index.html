The Cautious Investigator — Exploring from Cover

Feline responses to unfamiliar objects, safe retreats and individual variation.

Research behind the infographic

  • Cats need protection from perceived dangers, including unfamiliar environments; a secure retreat helps them feel protected. [1]
  • A safe place may be a cardboard box or carrier, with surrounding sides and the ability to enter and leave; raised safe places are often preferred. [1]
  • Cats use smell to evaluate their surroundings; unfamiliar smells or sounds can be stressful. [1]
  • The sudden appearance of a novel object may provoke a startle response; individual cats differ and fearful cats may react more strongly. [2]
  • Do not deliberately startle cats with surprise objects; frightened cats can associate the event with its location or person. [2]
  • The artwork illustrates possible observation, approach and retreat, not an obligatory sequence or a diagnosis; individual responses vary. [1] [2]
