The Feline Shoulder — Muscle Layers Around the Scapula

A focused atlas of superficial and deep shoulder muscle relationships in the domestic cat.

Research behind the infographic

  • The trapezius extends from dorsal midline to the scapular spine; rhomboideus lies deep to it and attaches to the dorsal scapular border. [1] [2]
  • Serratus ventralis is a deep fan-shaped muscle on the lateral trunk, with neck and thoracic parts. [1] [2]
  • Latissimus dorsi covers part of the thoracic wall, superficial to deeper shoulder structures. [1] [2]
  • The cat has a small clavicle in the brachiocephalicus at the shoulder. [1]
  • Pectoral muscles occupy the ventral chest; superficial and deep layers are present. [1] [2]
