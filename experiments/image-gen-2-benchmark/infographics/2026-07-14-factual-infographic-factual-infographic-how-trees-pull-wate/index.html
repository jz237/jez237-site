How Trees Pull Water Upward

A field-lab infographic showing how transpiration tension, water cohesion, and xylem anatomy move water from soil to a tree canopy, including stomata, pit membranes, and cavitation limits.

Research added after publication

  • Evaporation in leaves lowers water potential and creates tension in the xylem. Cohesion helps keep the water column connected so this pull can draw water upward from the roots. [1]
