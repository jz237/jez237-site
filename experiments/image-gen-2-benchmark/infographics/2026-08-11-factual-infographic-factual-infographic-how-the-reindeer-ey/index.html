How the Reindeer Eye Retunes for Winter

A museum-style anatomical and optical explainer comparing golden summer and blue winter tapeta in Arctic reindeer, with collagen packing, photon paths, seasonal light, and the sensitivity-acuity tradeoff.

Research added after publication

  • The reflecting layer behind the retina is golden in summer and blue in winter. Its winter state is associated with greater visual sensitivity under dim conditions. [1]
