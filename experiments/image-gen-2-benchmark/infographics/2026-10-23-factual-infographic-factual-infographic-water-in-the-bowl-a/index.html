Water in the Bowl and the Food

Dietary moisture versus drinking water, with typical food-format moisture and fresh-water access; no intake prescription.

Research behind the infographic

  • Cats take in water both by drinking and through moisture in their food. [1]
  • Wet food can contain up to 80% water; cats eating wet food may drink less, while dry-fed cats generally obtain more water by drinking. [1]
  • Dry cat food contains 6–10% water; canned food contains at least 75% water. These are typical food-format values, not feeding ratios. [2]
  • Cats should always have access to clean fresh water, even when eating wet food. [1] [2] [3]
  • Wet food can help increase water intake; a diet should still be complete and balanced. [1] [2] [3]
  • The bowl alone does not measure total water intake because food contributes water. [1]
  • If dehydration is suspected, contact a veterinarian promptly; this is general education rather than an individualized intake prescription. [1]
