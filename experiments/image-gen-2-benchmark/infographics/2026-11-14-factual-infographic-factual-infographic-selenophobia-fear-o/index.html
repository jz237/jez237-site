Selenophobia — Fear of the Moon or Moonlight

Named fear of the moon or moonlight, symptoms, avoidance and CBT support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Selenophobia is an intense fear of the moon or moonlight; it is described as a specific phobia, not a separate named diagnostic category. [1]
  • Thinking about the moon can provoke anxiety; seeing the moon and nighttime outings can be avoided. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms can include palpitations, trembling, dizziness and chest tightness; symptoms differ between people. [2]
  • People may avoid looking at the moon or going out at night; perceived threat does not mean the moon or moonlight presents a real threat. [1]
  • CBT is a treatment for phobias; seek a health professional if symptoms affect everyday life. [2]
