Pediophobia — Fear of Dolls

Named fear of dolls with photographic trigger proximity, clinical context and evidence-based help.

Research behind the infographic

  • Pediophobia is an established descriptive name meaning fear of dolls. [1]
  • Humanlike doll faces can be experienced as uncanny or unnerving; this is a subjective reaction, not evidence that a doll is alive or dangerous. [1]
  • A phobia may cause anxiety or panic on contact or even when thinking about the feared object (anticipatory anxiety). [3]
  • Body alarm can include palpitations, trembling, dizziness and chest tightness. [2]
  • Avoidance can organise and limit daily life; avoiding contact is characteristic of phobias. [3]
  • CBT is a treatment for phobias; gradual exposure/desensitisation can be part of treatment, with specialist support. [2] [3]
  • Seek professional help if fear affects daily life. [2] [3]
