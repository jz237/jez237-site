Arginine — The Ammonia Detoxification Link

Feline dietary arginine dependence and its role in ammonia detoxification, explained with a simplified metabolic schematic.

Research behind the infographic

  • Arginine is an essential amino acid and a building block of proteins; cats require it in their diet. [1] [2]
  • Protein breakdown releases ammonia; arginine participates in ammonia detoxification. [1] [2]
  • The urea cycle converts ammonia to urea, with arginine as an important intermediate. [2]
  • Cats have limited endogenous arginine synthesis and depend on dietary supply. [1] [2]
  • Arginine deficiency can cause ammonia accumulation with vomiting, neurological signs and potentially death. [1] [2]
  • A carefully formulated complete feline diet accounts for these special nutrient requirements; an all-meat diet is not balanced. [1]
