Inside a Hydrothermal Vent Chimney

A source-verified cutaway of how seawater circulates through fractured oceanic crust, becomes hot mineral-rich vent fluid, builds a black-smoker chimney through cooling and mineral precipitation, and supports chemosynthetic ecosystems at fluid-seawater mixing zones.

Research added after publication

  • Hot seawater circulating through the crust carries dissolved minerals. When the vent fluid cools against seawater, minerals precipitate, forming a chimney and sometimes a dark plume. [1]
