How Magnetotactic Bacteria Follow Earth’s Magnetic Field

A geomicrobiology workbench infographic showing how magnetite or greigite magnetosome chains orient swimming bacteria while flagella and chemical sensing help them navigate sediment oxygen gradients.

Research added after publication

  • Magnetosomes contain tiny crystals of magnetic iron minerals. Arranged in a chain, they act like an internal compass that aligns the cells with a magnetic field as they swim. [1]
