Insect Phobia — The Moth Beyond the Glass

An illustrated account of insect-focused specific phobia and source-backed support, with an ordinary-scale moth beyond glass as a fictional hero situation.

Research behind the infographic

  • Insects can be the focus of a specific animal phobia. [2]
  • Phobia fear is intense and out of proportion to the actual danger; it is more than ordinary dislike. [1]
  • Anticipating or thinking about the feared object can provoke intense anxiety; encountering it can also trigger anxiety. [1] [3]
  • People with phobias may actively avoid feared objects and situations or endure them with intense anxiety. [1]
  • Symptoms can include palpitations, chest tightness, nausea, trembling, dizziness, and severe worry; symptoms vary. [2]
  • Avoidance of the feared object can limit daily activities and lead people to organise life around avoiding their fear. [3]
  • CBT teaches different ways of thinking, behaving, and reacting to reduce fear and anxiety. [1]
  • Exposure therapy is a CBT method particularly effective for phobias; simple-phobia treatment can involve gradually becoming exposed to the feared object. [1] [3]
  • Treatment is individualized; a qualified mental health professional can help plan next steps. [1] [3]
  • Breathing exercises, talking with a trusted person, and support groups can help manage symptoms. [2]
  • Seek professional help when phobia symptoms interfere with everyday life. [2] [3]
