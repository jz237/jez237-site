Planted Aquarium Nitrogen Cycle

A real-world aquarium-care infographic showing how waste becomes ammonia, nitrite, and nitrate, how nitrifying bacteria and plants interact, and why testing and water changes matter.

Research added after publication

  • Nitrification happens in two biological steps: ammonia is oxidized to nitrite, then nitrite is oxidized to nitrate. It requires oxygen; nitrate production does not mean nitrogen has left the aquarium. [1]
