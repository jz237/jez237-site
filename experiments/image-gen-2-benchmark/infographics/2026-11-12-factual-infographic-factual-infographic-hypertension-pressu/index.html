Hypertension — Pressure That Can Go Unnoticed

Arterial pressure, silent presentation, organ risks, measurement and evidence-based care with emergency context.

Research behind the infographic

  • Hypertension means persistently high pressure of blood against artery walls; arteries carry blood away from the heart. [1] [2]
  • Systolic pressure is the pressure during heart pumping; diastolic pressure is measured between beats while the heart fills. [1]
  • High blood pressure usually has no symptoms; measurements, often repeated over visits and with a validated home monitor, help establish diagnosis. [2]
  • High blood pressure can damage heart, brain and kidneys over time and increases risk of heart attack, heart failure, stroke and kidney disease. [1] [2]
  • Risk factors include family history, increasing age, excess sodium, inactivity and excess alcohol; multiple factors can contribute. [2]
  • Care can include a heart-healthy DASH eating pattern, limiting sodium and alcohol, physical activity and medicines prescribed to the individual. [1] [2]
  • Pressure higher than 180 systolic and/or higher than 120 diastolic with chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness, vision change or difficulty speaking is an emergency: call 911; without symptoms, contact a healthcare professional. [2]
