Iatrophobia — Fear of Doctors

An imagined close clinical encounter explains the established fear name, symptoms, avoidance and CBT support without portraying clinicians as dangerous.

Research behind the infographic

  • Iatrophobia means fear of doctors. [1] [3]
  • Iatrophobia can involve extreme fear of doctors or medical tests, beyond the actual danger; it is described as a specific phobic disorder. [3]
  • Phobia symptoms can include severe worry, palpitations, dizziness, nausea and trembling. [2]
  • Fear may lead people to avoid necessary medical attention, even when sick. [3]
  • Phobias may relate to frightening experiences; causes are not always clear. [2] [3]
  • CBT is a talking therapy used to treat phobias; seek professional help when symptoms affect life. [2]
  • Talking to a trusted person and relaxation breathing can help manage phobia symptoms. [2]
