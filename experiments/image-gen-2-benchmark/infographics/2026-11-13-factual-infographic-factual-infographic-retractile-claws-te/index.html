Retractile Claws — Tendon Pull and Elastic Return

Normal forepaw digit anatomy and the tendon–ligament mechanism of claw movement.

Research behind the infographic

  • In domestic cat forepaw digits 2–5, the distal phalanx overlaps the middle phalanx when retracted. [1]
  • Elastic ligaments passively retract the claw; the lateral ligament is especially important, with a pair of dorsal elastic ligaments. [1]
  • Deep digital flexor muscle contraction transmits pull through its tendon to the flexor process of the distal phalanx, protruding the claw and stretching elastic ligaments. [1] [2]
  • Retraction protects claws from wear when not in use. [1] [2]
  • The distal and middle phalanges articulate at the distal interphalangeal joint. [1]
