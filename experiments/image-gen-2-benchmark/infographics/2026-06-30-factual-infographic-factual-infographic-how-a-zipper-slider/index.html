How a Zipper Slider Interlocks Teeth

Explains how a real zipper slider guides separated teeth into an interlocked chain, with conservative engineering labels and repair-bench context.

Research added after publication

  • A zipper has two rows of matching elements, a slider and fabric tapes. Moving the slider brings the elements together so they mesh; reversing it separates the rows. [1]
