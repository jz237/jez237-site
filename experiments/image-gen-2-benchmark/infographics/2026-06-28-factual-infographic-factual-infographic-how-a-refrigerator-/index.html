How a Refrigerator Door Seal Works

Shows how a flexible magnetic refrigerator door gasket compresses against the cabinet, limits warm-air leaks, and can develop condensation risk when dirty, twisted, or poorly seated.

Research added after publication

  • Magnetic refrigerator seals can use an extruded magnetic strip shaped for the door gasket. The strip is part of the seal; the gasket is the material that closes the gap around the door. [1]
