How the Wood Frog Survives Whole-Body Freezing

A real-world non-fiction physiology infographic showing how wood frogs coordinate extracellular ice formation, liver-derived glucose, winter urea accumulation, cellular water balance, metabolic depression, and controlled thawing—and why tolerance varies among populations and conditions.

Research added after publication

  • The studied subarctic wood frogs accumulated urea before freezing and mobilized glucose after freezing began. These solutes help protect tissues during freezing. [1]
