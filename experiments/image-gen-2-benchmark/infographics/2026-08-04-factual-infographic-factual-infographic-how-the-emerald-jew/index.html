How the Emerald Jewel Wasp Hijacks a Cockroach Escape Circuit

A direct-canvas, insect-first natural-history infographic showing how the emerald jewel wasp uses two precisely targeted stings to transiently affect the forelegs and then suppress spontaneous cockroach escape behavior without simply immobilizing the host, followed by antenna-guided walking and the parasitoid life cycle.
