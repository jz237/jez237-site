How a Heat Dome Works

A real-world weather infographic explaining how a persistent high-pressure ridge can suppress clouds and mixing, steer storms around a region, and allow repeated solar heating and warm nights to build dangerous heat.

Archive source notes

Conservative general meteorology: heat domes involve persistent high pressure/ridging, sinking/compressing air, reduced cloud cover and mixing, jet-stream blocking patterns, and cumulative surface heating; not a literal glass dome.
