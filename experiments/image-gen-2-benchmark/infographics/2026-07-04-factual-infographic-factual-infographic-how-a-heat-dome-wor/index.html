How a Heat Dome Works

A real-world weather infographic explaining how a persistent high-pressure ridge can suppress clouds and mixing, steer storms around a region, and allow repeated solar heating and warm nights to build dangerous heat.

Research added after publication

  • A heat dome is associated with a persistent region of high pressure. Descending air can warm, and the resulting weather pattern can keep very hot conditions in place for days or longer. [1]
