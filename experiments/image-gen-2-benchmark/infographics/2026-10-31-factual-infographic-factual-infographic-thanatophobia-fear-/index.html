Thanatophobia — Fear of Death

Artwork-led educational chart explaining the established name, death-related reminders, anxiety symptoms, avoidance and professional support without presenting ordinary mortality awareness as illness.

Research behind the infographic

  • Thanatophobia is an established term for fear of death; the displayed definition is Fear of death. [1]
  • Death anxiety is studied across multiple mental health conditions; the term is not being presented as a separate diagnosis in this educational chart. [2] [4]
  • Awareness of mortality is common; distress and disruption of daily life warrant assessment rather than assuming all death-related worry is illness. [4] [3]
  • Death-related conversations and news can act as reminders that provoke death anxiety; thoughts of one’s own death can be feared. [4]
  • Phobic anxiety may include palpitations, dizziness and trembling; symptoms differ between people. [3]
  • Avoidance of death-related reminders can be an unhelpful coping behavior maintaining death anxiety; short-term relief should not be equated with lasting recovery. [2] [4]
  • Perceived threat can exceed the current situation: a reminder or frightening thought is not itself evidence of imminent death. [4]
  • CBT can reduce death anxiety by addressing unhelpful beliefs and avoidance; seek individualized professional assessment when fear disrupts life. [2] [3] [4]
  • Any exposure work should be planned collaboratively with a trained clinician, using safe gradual steps rather than forced confrontation; normal medical care should be maintained. [4] [3]
