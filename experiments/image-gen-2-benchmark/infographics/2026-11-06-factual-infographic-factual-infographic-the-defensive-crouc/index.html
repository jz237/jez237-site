The Defensive Crouch in Context

Focused atlas of defensive feline postural signals and contextual interpretation.

Research behind the infographic

  • A defensive cat may crouch low with its head tucked and tail curved around or tucked under its body. [1] [2]
  • Flattened sideways or backward ears and wide eyes with dilated pupils can accompany defensive postures. [1] [2]
  • Hissing and raised fur may accompany fear-related defensive behavior; not every sign must be present. [1] [2]
  • Read body posture, face, ears and tail together and consider preceding events; a pose alone does not establish its cause. [2] [1]
  • Fear-related aggression can escalate if a cat cannot escape; do not touch, restrain or punish a cat displaying defensive aggression; give space until calm. [2]
  • Pain or medical problems may contribute to aggression; new or persistent changes warrant veterinary assessment. [1] [2]
