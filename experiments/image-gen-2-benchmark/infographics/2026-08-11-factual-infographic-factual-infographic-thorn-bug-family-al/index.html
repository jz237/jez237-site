Thorn Bug Family Alarm

A direct-canvas natural-history infographic explaining how real thorn bug nymphs coordinate substrate-borne vibration bursts through their host twig to recruit maternal defense.

Research added after publication

  • Thornbug offspring can send coordinated vibrations through the host plant when disturbed. Studies of Umbonia crassicornis report that these signals evoke a defensive response from the mother. [1]
