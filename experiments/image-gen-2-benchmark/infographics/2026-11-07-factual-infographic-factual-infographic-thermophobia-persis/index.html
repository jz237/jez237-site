Thermophobia — Persistent Fear of Heat

Photographic educational chart explaining heat fear, variable anxiety symptoms, avoidance, CBT and genuine heat-health precautions.

Research behind the infographic

  • Thermophobia is an established descriptive term for fear of heat; a published case uses the name for fear involving hot food. This is a descriptive fear name, not a claim of a separate diagnostic category. [1]
  • Heat-related situations and hot objects can trigger anxiety; the term meaning persistent fear of heat was additionally checked directly in the medically edited RxList dictionary. [1]
  • Phobias can involve severe worry, palpitations, trembling, dizziness and fear of losing control; symptoms differ between people. [2]
  • Avoidance of feared circumstances can bring short-term relief; the cited case reports avoidance and spontaneous subsiding of symptoms, not a universal mechanism for all patients. [1]
  • Phobias can interfere with daily life; seek professional help if symptoms affect life. [1] [2]
  • CBT is a talking therapy used to treat phobias. [2]
  • Heat can cause actual illness; stay cool and hydrated, use shade and cooling, and take local heat-risk advice seriously. [3]
  • Overheating symptoms can include heavy sweating, dizziness, headache, weakness and nausea; physical symptoms must not simply be assumed to be anxiety. [3] [2]
