How Termite Mounds Breathe Without Fans

Explains how termite mounds buffer heat, humidity, oxygen, and carbon dioxide through porous walls, galleries, chimney passages, and temperature-driven airflow.

Research added after publication

  • In the mounds studied, day-to-night temperature changes drove reversing airflows that helped flush carbon dioxide from the nest. The result describes a measured mechanism, not a single design used by every termite species. [1]
