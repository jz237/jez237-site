How Basalt Columns Crack Into Polygons

A volcanology-bench infographic showing how cooling and thermal contraction organize fractures into polygonal basalt columns.

Research added after publication

  • Cooling volcanic rock contracts. Fractures form and advance through the cooling body, dividing it into columns that are often hexagonal but can have other polygonal cross sections. [1]
