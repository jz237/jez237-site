How Solar Sails Get Thrust From Sunlight

A real-world physics infographic explaining how reflective solar sails use photon momentum for tiny continuous thrust without onboard propellant.

Archive source notes

Conservative summary of standard solar-sail physics: photons carry momentum; reflection changes photon momentum and transfers a small continuous force to the sail; steering changes sail angle; sunlight weakens with distance from the Sun.
