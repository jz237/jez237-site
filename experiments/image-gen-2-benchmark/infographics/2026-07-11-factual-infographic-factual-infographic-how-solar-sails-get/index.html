How Solar Sails Get Thrust From Sunlight

A real-world physics infographic explaining how reflective solar sails use photon momentum for tiny continuous thrust without onboard propellant.

Research added after publication

  • Photons carry momentum even though they have no rest mass. When they hit a reflective sail, they transfer momentum to the spacecraft; the small pushes add up over a large illuminated area. [1]
