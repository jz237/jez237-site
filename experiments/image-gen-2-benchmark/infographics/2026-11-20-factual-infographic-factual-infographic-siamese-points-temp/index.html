Siamese Points — Temperature and Growing Fur

A focused atlas of temperature-sensitive pigment production and regrowth in Siamese-pattern coats.

Research behind the infographic

  • Siamese pigmentation is associated with variants of the tyrosinase gene TYR. [2]
  • Pigment production in Siamese cats is inefficient at warmer trunk skin temperatures and occurs more readily in cooler skin. [1]
  • The face, ears, legs and tail are normally cooler and darker than the central body in a seal-point Siamese. [1]
  • When body fur is lost in cold conditions, cooler exposed skin can produce darker replacement fur as it grows back. [1]
