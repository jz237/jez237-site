Ear Positions — Read the Whole Cat

Focused feline ear-posture atlas with facial and body context; signals are qualified, never fixed emotional diagnoses.

Research behind the infographic

  • Ear position is interpreted together with eyes, whiskers, posture, tail and context; these are contextual cues, not a guaranteed emotion or diagnosis. [1] [2]
  • Upright ears with half-closed eyes can accompany a relaxed expression. [2]
  • Forward ears and open eyes can indicate attention or anxiety, rather than reliably meaning happiness. [2]
  • Flattened ears with whiskers drawn against the face can accompany fear; fear can also involve a low crouch and a tucked or closely wrapped tail. [1] [2]
  • Ears back and flat with whiskers directed forward can accompany defensive or aggressive communication; ears moving backward with tail lashing can precede petting-induced aggression. [1] [2]
  • When ears move back and the tail lashes during petting, stop petting and allow a cooling-down period without physical contact. Avoid punishment, restraint or uninvited handling. [1]
