Entomophobia — When an Insect Feels Too Close

Photographic entomophobia education with immediate natural-scale insect proximity and clinical context.

Research behind the infographic

  • Entomophobia means fear of insects; seeing or thinking about insects can provoke extreme fear or anxiety. [1]
  • Entomophobia is a specific phobia; fear is out of proportion to likely real-life harm, not proof that an insect encounter is dangerous. [1]
  • Encounters indoors or outdoors, images, and fears of bites or stings can be triggers. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms can include palpitations, trembling, chest tightness and dizziness. [2]
  • Avoidance may restrict outdoor exercise, events or leaving home. [1]
  • CBT is a recommended treatment for phobias; a therapist helps examine thoughts, feelings and actions and change avoidance in an individualized plan. [2] [3]
  • Seek professional help when symptoms affect daily life. [2]
