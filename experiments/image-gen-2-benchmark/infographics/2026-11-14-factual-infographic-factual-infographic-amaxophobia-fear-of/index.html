Amaxophobia — Fear of Driving or Riding in a Vehicle

A source-backed photographic infographic about amaxophobia and evidence-based support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Amaxophobia means fear of driving or riding in a vehicle; it is a specific phobic disorder, not a separate stand-alone diagnostic category. [1]
  • Thinking about a vehicle journey may trigger anticipatory anxiety; feared outcomes may include a crash or injury. [1] [3]
  • Phobia symptoms can include palpitations, sweating and trembling; these are bodily anxiety responses. [2] [3]
  • Avoiding feared situations can restrict everyday life and continual avoidance can worsen the problem. [3]
  • Phobias involve an exaggerated or unrealistic sense of danger; feeling endangered is not proof that a crash is imminent. [3] [1]
  • CBT can help phobias; gradual exposure may be used with professional support. Seek help if symptoms affect daily life. [2] [3]
