Pacific blue tang

Pacific blue tang (Paracanthurus hepatus) reef biology and aquarium care

Research behind the infographic

  • Indo-Pacific tropical coral reefs, often near branching coral and currents. [1] [2]
  • Blue oval body, dark markings and yellow tail; adults average 25–31 cm. [1]
  • Consumes algae and zooplankton; shelters in coral and may live in groups. [1]
  • Sharp tail-base spine can cause painful injury; surgeonfish can clash in aquaria. [1] [2]
