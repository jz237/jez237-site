Pogonophobia — A Beard Too Close

A photographic guide to beard fear, body alarm and professional support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Pogonophobia means extreme fear of beards. [1]
  • Real or artificial beards, photos, videos, mustaches and other facial hair can trigger severe anxiety or panic. [1]
  • Unpredictable encounters can interfere with work and public or social life. [1]
  • A beard can feel threatening to the person with the phobia; the fear does not establish actual danger from the beard or its wearer. [1]
  • Phobias can cause palpitations, trembling, dizziness and chest tightness. [2]
  • CBT is a treatment for phobias; seek professional help when symptoms affect life. [2]
  • Specific phobic disorders are anxiety disorders. [1]
