Haphephobia — A Hand Too Close

Touch-related anxiety, bodily symptoms, avoidance, distinction from allodynia and CBT support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Haphephobia means intense, overwhelming fear of being touched; touch from friends or family can also cause distress. [1]
  • Haphephobia is an anxiety disorder and differs from allodynia, physical pain from touch. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms may include severe anxiety, palpitations, trembling and dizziness. [2]
  • Anxiety may disrupt everyday activities and cause avoidance of encounters or leaving home. [3]
  • CBT is a treatment for phobias; professional help is appropriate when symptoms affect life. [2]
