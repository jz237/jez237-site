How Bioluminescent Plankton Makes Waves Glow

A non-fiction marine science infographic explaining why disturbed plankton-rich seawater can flash blue in breaking waves.

Research added after publication

  • Some dinoflagellates emit blue light when disturbed by breaking waves or other water motion. Not every bloom or red tide glows; it depends on which organisms are present. [1]
