How Retroreflective Sign Vinyl Sends Headlights Back

Real-world sign-material science explaining how microprismatic or glass-bead retroreflective sheeting sends headlight rays back toward the driver instead of glowing on its own.

Research added after publication

  • Traffic-sign sheeting uses tiny glass beads or cube-corner reflectors to send much of the incoming light back toward its source. That is why a sign can appear bright to a driver whose eyes are close to the headlights. [1]
