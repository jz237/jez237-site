Horseshoe Crab Blue Blood

Copper-based oxygen transport and endotoxin detection in horseshoe crab blood.

Research behind the infographic

  • Horseshoe crabs are marine arthropods with an exoskeleton and jointed appendages. [1]
  • Copper-containing hemocyanin carries oxygen and gives horseshoe crab blood its blue color. [2]
  • Blood clotting responds to bacterial endotoxins; LAL is used to test medical products for endotoxins. [2]
  • Scientists are exploring synthetic alternatives to LAL. [1] [2]
