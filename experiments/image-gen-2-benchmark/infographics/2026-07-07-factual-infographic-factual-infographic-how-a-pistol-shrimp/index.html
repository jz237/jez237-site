How a Pistol Shrimp Snap Makes a Cavitation Flash

A real-world marine biology and physics infographic explaining how a snapping shrimp claw creates a fast water jet, cavitation bubble, pressure pulse, and tiny flash.

Research added after publication

  • The closing claw drives water fast enough to form a cavitation bubble. The bubble collapse, rather than the two claws striking each other, produces the characteristic pressure pulse and can emit a brief flash of light. [1]
