How a Honeybee Waggle Dance Shares a Food Location

Explains how a worker honeybee's waggle run, return loops, dance angle, and waggle duration/vigor communicate food direction and general distance to nestmates.

Research added after publication

  • In the waggle dance, the orientation of the waggle run encodes direction relative to the sun, while the duration of the waggle run conveys distance. Returning foragers use the dance to recruit nestmates to a resource. [1]
