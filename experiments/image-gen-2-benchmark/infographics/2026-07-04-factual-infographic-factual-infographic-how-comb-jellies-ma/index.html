How Comb Jellies Make Traveling Rainbow Light

Explains how comb jellies produce traveling rainbow shimmer through structural light scattering from beating ciliary plates, contrasted with true bioluminescence.

Research added after publication

  • A comb jelly's beating cilia split incoming light into shimmering colors as it swims. This comb-row rainbow is an optical effect, distinct from biologically produced light. [1]
