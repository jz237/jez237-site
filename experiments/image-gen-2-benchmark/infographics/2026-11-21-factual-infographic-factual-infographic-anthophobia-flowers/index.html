Anthophobia — Flowers Too Close

Flower-related fear, body alarm, functional impact and professional treatment, distinguished from actual danger.

Research behind the infographic

  • Anthophobia means an extreme fear of flowers; any flower or specific types, and parts including petals, leaves or stems, may trigger fear. [1]
  • Anthophobia is described as a specific phobic disorder; this is a descriptive fear name, not a claim of a separate diagnostic category. [1]
  • Thinking about flowers can trigger anxiety; possible bodily symptoms include palpitations, sweating, trembling and shortness of breath. [1] [2]
  • Phobic fear concerns something unlikely to cause harm; intense felt alarm is not itself proof of actual danger. [1]
  • Avoiding flowers can restrict work and social life and make leaving home difficult. [1]
  • A mental health professional can guide gradual exposure therapy, often combined with CBT to change perceptions and responses. [1]
  • Seek professional help when phobia symptoms affect daily life. [1] [2]
