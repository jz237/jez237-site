Cat Atlas — Inside the Cat

An artistic atlas of normal feline anatomy, circulation, digestion, urinary function and paw-and-claw structure.

Research behind the infographic

  • The liver lies immediately caudal to the diaphragm; the stomach lies caudal to the liver and intestinal loops occupy the abdomen. [1]
  • Blood returns from the body to the right heart, travels to the lungs, returns to the left heart, and is pumped to the body. [2]
  • The heart pumps blood while the lungs exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide. [2]
  • The digestive tract breaks down food, absorbs nutrients and moves remaining waste toward elimination. [3]
  • The urinary system includes two kidneys, ureters, a bladder and a urethra; it removes wastes and regulates water and electrolytes. [4] [5]
  • Cats have retractile curved claws for grip and padded paws that cushion contact. [6]
