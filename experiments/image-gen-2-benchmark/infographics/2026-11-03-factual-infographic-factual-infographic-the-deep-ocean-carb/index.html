The Deep Ocean Carbon Pump

How phytoplankton, marine snow, and zooplankton pellets move organic carbon into the deep ocean and support deep-sea life.

Research behind the infographic

  • Sunlit phytoplankton use photosynthesis to convert carbon dioxide into organic carbon. [2]
  • Phytoplankton can aggregate into sinking marine snow. [1]
  • Zooplankton eat phytoplankton and produce carbon-containing fecal pellets that sink. [1] [2]
  • Sinking particles transport organic carbon from surface waters toward the deep ocean. [1] [2]
  • Marine snow provides food to deep-sea animals and seafloor organisms. [1]
  • Some carbon reaches deep waters and sediments, where it can remain stored for long periods. [1] [2]
