How Honeybees Map a Flower Patch

How successful western honeybee foragers communicate an approximate sun-relative direction and distance to a food source through waggle-run orientation, duration, repeated circuits, and close-range sensory cues.

Research added after publication

  • The angle of a returning bee's waggle run conveys direction relative to the sun; the duration conveys distance. Recruits use that information to search for food. [1]
