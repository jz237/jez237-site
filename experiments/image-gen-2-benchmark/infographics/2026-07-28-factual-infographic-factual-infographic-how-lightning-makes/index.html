How Lightning Makes Fulgurites

A non-fiction visual explanation of how a brief lightning current can locally melt suitable geological material, which rapidly quenches into variable glassy fulgurite structures.

Research added after publication

  • A lightning discharge through suitable sand can briefly melt material around its path. The melt cools into a glassy fulgurite, preserving some of the shape of the route the discharge took. [1]
