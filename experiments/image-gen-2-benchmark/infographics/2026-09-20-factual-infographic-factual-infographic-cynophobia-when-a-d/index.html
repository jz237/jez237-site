Research behind the infographic

  • Fear of dogs is an example of a specific phobia. [1]
  • A phobia involves intense fear or aversion that is out of proportion to the actual danger presented by the object or situation. [1] [2]
  • Encountering a feared object can bring immediate intense anxiety, while anticipation can also produce anxiety before an encounter. [1] [2]
  • Anxiety can include rapid heartbeat, shortness of breath, muscle tension, restlessness, and difficulty concentrating; symptoms vary between people. [2]
  • People with phobias may actively avoid the feared object or endure unavoidable encounters with intense anxiety. [1]
  • Anxiety disorders can interfere with daily activities, work, school, relationships, or overall well-being. [2]
  • Psychotherapy is the primary treatment approach for phobias, and cognitive behavioral therapy is a well-established treatment. [1]
  • Exposure therapy is a CBT method that gradually addresses feared situations and can help people re-engage with activities they have avoided. [1] [2]
  • Assessment and treatment planning should be done with a qualified health professional and tailored to the individual. [1] [2]
