Chronophobia — Extreme Fear of Time or Time Passing

Source-backed explanation of chronophobia, reminders, variable phobia symptoms, avoidance and professional support.

Research behind the infographic

  • Chronophobia means an extreme fear of time or time passing. [1]
  • Chronophobia is a named fear described clinically within specific phobias; the name does not imply a separate diagnostic code. [1]
  • Thinking about time passing, getting older or mortality can evoke intense discomfort or dread; some people obsess over clocks or calendar days. [1]
  • Phobia symptoms can include palpitations, trembling and dizziness; symptoms vary between people. [2]
  • People with specific phobias may avoid reminders of what they fear; chronophobia can involve social isolation and interference with relationships. [1]
  • Time cannot be stopped; a calendar or clock is not itself an immediate danger even when the associated alarm feels intense. [1]
  • Psychotherapy can help with chronophobia; cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) is a treatment for phobias. [1] [2]
  • Seek professional help when symptoms affect daily life; talking to someone trusted and relaxation breathing may help manage symptoms. [2]
