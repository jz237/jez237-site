Banggai cardinalfish

Natural history, appearance, mouthbrooding and aquarium care of Banggai cardinalfish.

Research behind the infographic

  • Silver fish with black bars, white spots, elongated fins and adult length up to 8 cm [1]
  • Native Banggai archipelago shallow coral reefs and seagrass [1] [2]
  • Carnivore eats small crustaceans; varied meaty foods in captivity [1] [2]
  • Male mouthbroods eggs; pairs may be aggressive towards conspecifics [1] [2]
  • Mixed reef with non-aggressive small fish and hiding places; prefer captive bred [2]
