How Red Sprites Flash Above Thunderstorms

A field-station science display explains how lightning-driven electric-field changes briefly excite thin upper-atmosphere nitrogen, producing vast red sprites above thunderstorms.

Archive source notes

Conservative synthesis of established atmospheric-science descriptions of transient luminous events: sprites occur in the mesosphere above strong thunderstorms, are associated with major lightning-driven electric-field changes, glow mainly through excited nitrogen emissions, and are distinct from blue jets and elves.
