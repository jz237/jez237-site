How Red Sprites Flash Above Thunderstorms

A field-station science display explains how lightning-driven electric-field changes briefly excite thin upper-atmosphere nitrogen, producing vast red sprites above thunderstorms.

Research added after publication

  • Sprites are one type of transient luminous event above thunderstorms. Their brief flashes occur high above the clouds, rather than following the near-ground path of an ordinary cloud-to-ground lightning strike. [1]
