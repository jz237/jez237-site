How a Brinicle Grows Beneath Sea Ice

A real-world, non-fiction polar-ocean infographic showing how salt rejected during young sea-ice growth drains as cold dense brine and freezes surrounding seawater into a hollow tubular ice stalactite.

Research added after publication

  • Cold dense brine flows down from sea ice. Seawater around the flow freezes into a hollow tube, through which brine can continue descending. [1]
