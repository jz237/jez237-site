Milk Lactose — Why the Saucer Can Upset Digestion

Feline milk lactose digestion and general nutrition context.

Research behind the infographic

  • Many cats are lactose intolerant; dairy can cause gastrointestinal problems. [1] [2]
  • Lactose is milk sugar; intestinal lactase is needed to digest it. Many cats lack enough lactase. [2]
  • Drinking lactose-containing milk can cause vomiting, diarrhoea and stomach pain; not every cat becomes ill. [2]
  • Adult cats do not need milk when fed an appropriate complete balanced diet. [2]
  • Cats should always have clean fresh water available. [1] [2]
  • Reduced-lactose or lactose-free cat milk still adds calories and is often not a complete meal. [2]
