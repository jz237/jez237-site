Taurine — An Essential Dietary Link

A focused nutritional plate connecting limited feline taurine synthesis with dietary supply and normal retinal and heart-muscle needs, alongside complete-and-balanced food-label guidance.

Research behind the infographic

  • Cats have a very limited ability to synthesize taurine from cysteine; dietary taurine is essential. [2]
  • Low activities of cysteine dioxygenase and cysteinesulfinic acid decarboxylase limit feline taurine synthesis. The plate simplifies this to a limited internal supply, not zero synthesis. [2]
  • Taurine deficiency can cause central retinal degeneration and dilated cardiomyopathy; eye and heart studies illustrate the importance of adequate dietary taurine, not a disease diagnosis or treatment. [2]
  • Animal-source foodstuffs supply taurine; a meat ingredient alone does not establish the completeness of a diet. [1] [2]
  • Cats are obligate carnivores requiring a balance of multiple nutrients, not taurine alone. [1] [2]
  • Choose cat food with a nutritional adequacy statement indicating complete and balanced nutrition for the appropriate life stage. Ingredients and nutrient balance are distinct. [1]
  • Supplements are usually unnecessary with a complete balanced diet and should not be given without veterinary approval. [1]
