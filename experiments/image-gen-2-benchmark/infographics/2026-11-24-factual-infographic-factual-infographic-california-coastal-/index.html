California Coastal Upwelling

Wind-driven coastal upwelling and its ecological consequences.

Research behind the infographic

  • Alongshore winds can drive surface water offshore. [1]
  • Deeper cold nutrient-rich water rises to replace displaced surface water. [1]
  • California coastal upwelling supports marine productivity and fisheries. [1] [2]
  • Cold coastal water contributes to San Francisco summer fog. [1] [2]
