Longnose hawkfish

A species-specific atlas of diagnostic markings, perching anatomy, range, habitat, small-prey feeding and explicitly inferred aquarium compatibility cautions.

Research behind the infographic

  • Accepted species Oxycirrhites typus Bleeker 1857; Oxycirrhites morrisi and Oxycirrhites seftoni are synonyms. Oxycirrhitus typus is a documented alternate genus spelling. [3]
  • Whitish body with horizontal and vertical red bands forming a cross-hatch grid; slender compressed body and very long thin snout. [1] [2]
  • Tufts of cirri at dorsal spine tips; lower pectoral rays stout and elongated, used for perching. [1] [2]
  • To 13 cm (about 5.1 inches). [2]
  • Indo-Pacific and eastern Pacific marine range, including the Red Sea. [1] [3]
  • Perches on gorgonians or black coral; generally deeper reef habitat, with reported total depth range 5–100 m and shallow exceptions. [1] [2]
  • Feeds on zooplankton and small crustaceans; pointed mouth helps capture small prey. [4]
  • Aquarium implications, explicitly labeled inferences rather than tested universal compatibility: provide stable perching structure and suitable small meaty foods; small crustacean tankmates may be prey, so do not promise universal reef safety. Habitat association does not prove all corals or all companions are safe. No numeric tank minimum asserted. [1] [2] [4]
