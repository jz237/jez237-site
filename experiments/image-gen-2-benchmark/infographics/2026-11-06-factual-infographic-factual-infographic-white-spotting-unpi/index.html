White Spotting — Unpigmented Patches

An artwork-led cat coat atlas explaining melanocyte absence and KIT-associated white spotting, distinct from Dominant White.

Research behind the infographic

  • White spotting ranges from tiny white patches to extensive white areas; these are illustrative examples, not a genotype scale. [1]
  • White areas lack skin melanocytes, the pigment-producing cells; this differs from failure of pigment synthesis. [1]
  • An insertion of a full-length feline endogenous retrovirus in KIT intron 1 is associated with white spotting in cats. [1] [2]
  • A distinct shorter viral LTR insertion at KIT is associated with Dominant White; white spotting and Dominant White are distinct phenotypes. [1] [2]
