How a Whale Fall Feeds the Deep Sea

A direct-canvas deep-sea ecology infographic showing how whale tissue, enriched sediment, bone lipids, microbes, Osedax worms, and sulfur oxidation support overlapping communities around a whale fall.

Research added after publication

  • A whale carcass becomes both food and habitat. As organic material in the bones breaks down, sulfides can support microbes that use chemical energy and, in turn, the animals that depend on them. [1]
