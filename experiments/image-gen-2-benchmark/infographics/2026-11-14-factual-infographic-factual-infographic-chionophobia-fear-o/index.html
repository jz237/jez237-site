Chionophobia — Fear of Snow

Snow-related phobic fear with photographic subjective trigger proximity and clinical context.

Research behind the infographic

  • Chionophobia means intense fear of snow; Cleveland Clinic describes it as a natural-environment specific phobia, not a separate named diagnostic code. [1]
  • Seeing snow or wintry weather, or even thinking about light snowfall, can trigger severe anxiety. [1]
  • Phobias can produce palpitations, trembling and dizziness. [2]
  • Avoiding snow can mean staying indoors during winter and disruption of work and relationships. [1]
  • Phobic alarm can exceed the actual danger; a light snowfall can provoke severe anxiety even without immediate danger. This does not imply all winter conditions are safe. [1]
  • Phobias can involve anticipatory anxiety and an overwhelming urge to avoid the feared situation, limiting daily activities. [1] [3]
  • CBT is a treatment option; gradual exposure or desensitisation is used for simple phobias. A specialist can help; no single programme works for everyone. Seek professional help when fear disrupts daily life. [2] [3]
