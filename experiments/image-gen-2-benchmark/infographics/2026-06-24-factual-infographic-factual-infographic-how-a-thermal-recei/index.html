How a Thermal Receipt Fades

A real-world materials-science infographic explaining how direct thermal receipt paper forms marks through heat-activated coating chemistry and why heat, UV, abrasion, oils, plasticizers, solvents, humidity, and poor storage can fade it.

Research added after publication

  • A direct-thermal print can fade in sunlight or prolonged fluorescent light. The manufacturer also warns that plasticizers in some tapes and sleeves can fade the printed image, so the storage material matters as well as the light exposure. [1]
