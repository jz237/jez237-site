Ocellaris clownfish

An illustrated guide to ocellaris clownfish biology and aquarium considerations.

Research behind the infographic

  • Accepted name Amphiprion ocellaris; synonyms Amphiprion melanurus and Amphiprion bicolor [3] [1]
  • Orange fish with three white bars and fine black margins, rounded tail; maximum length 11 cm. [1]
  • Indo-Pacific shallow coral and rocky reefs; associates with host anemones. [1] [2]
  • Feeds on algae and small planktonic animals. [1] [2]
  • Territorial around host; dominant female and breeding male; captive breeding can reduce wild collection pressure. [1] [2]
