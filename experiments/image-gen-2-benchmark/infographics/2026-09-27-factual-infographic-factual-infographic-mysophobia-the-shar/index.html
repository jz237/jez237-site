Research behind the infographic

  • Mysophobia, also called germophobia, is an extreme fear of germs and contamination. [1]
  • An uncertain-cleanliness public surface can be a trigger for mysophobia. [1]
  • A phobia can produce fear out of proportion to the actual danger, without implying that all public surfaces are germ-free. [3]
  • Phobia symptoms can include intense anxiety, a noticeable heartbeat, dizziness, and trembling. [2]
  • People with specific phobias may avoid feared objects or situations. [3]
  • Avoidance can interfere with daily life; phobia treatment can help. [2] [3]
  • Cognitive behavioral therapy is an effective treatment for phobias. [2] [3]
  • Exposure therapy is a CBT approach that helps people engage with situations they have been avoiding; it can be planned with a qualified professional. [3]
  • A healthcare professional can evaluate phobia symptoms and discuss next steps if fear affects daily life. [2] [3]
