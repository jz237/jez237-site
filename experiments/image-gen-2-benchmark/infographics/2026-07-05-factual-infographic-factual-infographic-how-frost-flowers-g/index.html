How Frost Flowers Grow on Young Sea Ice

Explains how delicate frost flower ice crystals form over young sea ice in cold calm conditions and become salty by interacting with brine.

Research added after publication

  • Frost flowers are ice crystals, not plants. Water vapor deposits as solid ice on the sea-ice surface without first turning into liquid water. [1]
