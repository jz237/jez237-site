Pillow Lava — Underwater Budding Basalt

How basaltic pillow lobes form and accumulate underwater.

Research behind the infographic

  • Basalt entering water can form bulbous pillow lobes. [1] [2]
  • Water rapidly chills the outside into a glassy rind. [1] [2]
  • Continued lava supply expands a lobe until its rind cracks and a new lobe buds out. [1] [2]
  • Pillows accumulate in mounds and settle into gaps between earlier pillows. [1] [2]
  • As a pillow interior cools, joints can radiate inward from its surface. [2]
  • Ancient pillow basalts can indicate a formerly underwater setting. [1]
