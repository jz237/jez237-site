ABOUT THIS EXPERIMENT

Pictures to help you
ask better questions.

Start with what you see, then check the whole tank. Similar appearances can have different causes.

The guide separates visual comparisons from care and treatment preparation. It does not confirm a diagnosis or prescribe a medication. A fish-health professional may need water tests, microscopy or other examinations.

The opening montage, symptom pictures, texture close-ups and the ich, freshwater velvet and cottony-growth galleries are AI educational illustrations, not confirmed patient photographs. They show possible appearances, not proof of a disease. The remaining condition-library entries still have image spaces to fill.

No photo upload, image analysis or external chatbot is part of this guide. You can optionally copy or download your observation notes.
