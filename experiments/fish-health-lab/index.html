THE IDEA WE’RE TESTING

Better questions.
Not false certainty.

A sick fish rarely comes with a neat diagnosis. A useful guide should make the next step clearer, not turn a photograph into a confident guess.

01 / LOOK

Start with visible clues

Choose drawings that resemble the change. Use your own photo as a side-by-side reference. Keep normal markings and species anatomy in mind.

02 / CONTEXT

Bring in the whole tank

Fresh or salt water, breathing, timing, new arrivals and actual water tests can change which explanations are plausible.

03 / COMPARE

Keep look-alikes visible

See which clues fit, which do not, and what professional test could distinguish them. We deliberately don’t invent confidence percentages.

04 / ASK

A better AI second opinion

Build a structured case, copy it, then take it to your preferred AI or a fish-health professional. You choose whether to attach a photo there.

What works in this version

Visual symptom selection, a reference-based comparison matcher, water and urgency checks, a searchable library, a private photo preview, and exportable case notes.

What this version does not do

It does not run an AI model on your photo, identify a pathogen, prescribe a treatment, or store a medical record. The matcher is not clinically validated. An AI handoff opens a separate service, whose own privacy terms apply.

Where we could take it next

  • A permission-based library of real, professionally identified cases, including mild and unusual presentations.
  • Optional AI photo observations that ask follow-up questions instead of declaring a diagnosis.
  • A side-by-side “why not this?” comparison and a concise case summary for a fish-health professional.
  • Species-specific and reef-invertebrate considerations, reviewed by an aquatic veterinarian.
