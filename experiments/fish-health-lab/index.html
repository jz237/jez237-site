THE IDEA WE’RE TESTING

Learn to see
the differences.

Start with pictures, compare the look-alikes, then bring in the tank history and water readings. The aim is a clearer next step—not a confident guess from a photograph.

01 / LOOK

Start with the pictures

The freshwater ich example has six reference illustrations: whole-fish guide, different fish, close-up, subtle appearance, marked changes and look-alikes.

02 / COMPARE

Choose what you actually see

Separate grains, fine dust, cottony growth, pale patches and larger growths can suggest different questions. Choosing a picture does not confirm a disease.

03 / CONTEXT

Check the whole tank

Water type, breathing, timing, new arrivals and actual water tests matter. Few visible spots do not guarantee a mild illness.

04 / NEXT STEP

What works in this version

A complete six-image freshwater ich walkthrough, a 15-entry comparison library, water and urgency checks, side-by-side reference comparisons, and optional downloadable observation notes.

What the pictures cannot establish

These are educational illustrations, not confirmed patient photographs. Pattern matching does not identify a pathogen. Professional examination, microscopy or other testing may be needed. The guide is not clinically validated and does not prescribe medication.

Where we could take it next
