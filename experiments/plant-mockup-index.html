Plant Progress

Every plant in the yard, tracked through the season. Click any card to see the full timeline.

Dwarf Peach Tree
Prunus persica, dwarf variety
From swelling buds to full pink bloom, a small tree with a surprisingly dramatic spring arc.
spring bloom fruit tree 7 photos
Season notes
Bare branches in late March, then an explosion of pink blossoms by mid-April. Peaches were treated with copper fungicide at pink bud stage. Leaf-out completes by late April. Watch for aphids in summer.
Rising Sun Redbud
Cercis canadensis 'The Rising Sun'
A spring flare plant, all neon leaves and electric bloom, dramatic in exactly the right way.
spring flare deciduous tree 7 photos
Season notes
The redbud kicks off with magenta blooms on bare wood, then the gold-orange leaves push through in waves. Each flush of new growth is a different color — starting lime, shifting through apricot, settling to chartreuse. One of the most photographed plants in the yard.
Shaina Japanese Maple
Acer palmatum 'Shaina'
A compact red mound in spring leaf-out, with that fine textured maple look showing against the brick and mulch.
spring leaf-out japanese maple 6 photos
Season notes
Shaina pushes deep red-maroon foliage that holds well through the season. Winter dieback required some cleanup pruning, but the spring flush came back strong and full.
Twombly's Red Sentinel
Acer palmatum 'Twombly's Red Sentinel'
A narrow red column with a spring glow, made even better by the gold-dusted green behind it.
spring glow japanese maple 3 photos
Season notes
The sentinel form stays tight and vertical. Spring brings a translucent red that catches light differently than Shaina's deeper maroon — these two maples complement each other perfectly in the yard.
Climbing Hydrangea
Hydrangea anomala subsp. petiolaris
A patient wall-claimer, slower to start, then suddenly full of intent.
spring wake-up vine 3 photos
Season notes
Climbing hydrangea takes its time establishing, but once it takes hold it covers walls and fences with a beautiful clinging habit. This year's spring push is looking vigorous.
Orange Rocket Barberry
Berberis thunbergii 'Orange Rocket'
A narrow upright shrub with spring color already coming in hot, reading more ember than green.
spring color shrub 4 photos
Season notes
One of the first shrubs to show real color in spring. The coral-to-red progression is visible week over week. Pairs well with the green backdrop of the side bed.
Pugster Butterfly Bushes
Buddleia 'Pugster' series
Early spring wake-up, woody structure first, then fresh green pushing back in around the cut-back framework.
spring wake-up shrub 3 photos
Season notes
Pugsters get cut back hard in late winter and look rough for a few weeks, then explode with new growth. Planted alongside orange milkweed and sedum in the same bed.
Grapevine
Vitis, trained on arbor
A current-year record of the arch and its spring training, with the vine beginning to push and the structure still visible.
spring training vine 6 photos
Season notes
The grapevine is trained onto an arbor gate structure. Spring means tying in new canes and watching the tannin-red leaves unfurl. By late summer it forms a full canopy over the arch.
Golden Architecture
Forsythia, trained on lattice
A trained burst of yellow against lattice, less shrub, more spring calligraphy.
early spring bloom shrub 2 photos
Season notes
Forsythia is the earliest color in the yard and the signal that pre-emergent timing is right. Prune right after bloom to keep the lattice training shape.
Lemony Lace Elderberry
Sambucus racemosa 'Lemony Lace'
A bright cut-leaf elderberry that reads almost electric in spring, airy in texture but surprisingly assertive once the light hits it.
spring bright shrub 3 photos
Season notes
Lemony Lace is one of those plants that looks better in person than in photos — the chartreuse-gold dissected foliage catches light in a way cameras don't fully capture.
Little Henry Sweetspire
Itea virginica 'Little Henry'
A compact native shrub, waking up in clean bright green and starting the season low, dense, and quietly promising.
spring wake-up native shrub 2 photos
Season notes
Little Henry is a quiet achiever — clean form, fragrant white flower spikes in early summer, and reliable red-purple fall color.
Stonehenge Skinny Yew
Taxus, columnar form
A very narrow columnar yew, still juvenile but already showing the tidy vertical habit that makes it useful as a clean evergreen line.
evergreen conifer 2 photos
Season notes
The skinny yew provides a vertical dark-green punctuation mark along the lattice. Slow growing, but that's the point — it won't outgrow its space.
White Rose Bush
Rosa, white variety
An old, vigorous white rose bush in the yard, full of fresh spring leaves and already pushing hard for the season.
spring wake-up shrub 1 photo
Season notes
Gets aphids early every summer. Treated with copper fungicide spray in spring alongside the peach tree.
