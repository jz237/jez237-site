Season Timeline
April 12, 2026
Full spring pink
▸
The dwarf peach is putting on a real spring show here, deep pink bloom clustered all along the branches while the first narrow green leaves start to slip out between the flowers. It still has that young upright shape, but the bloom is already strong enough to make it feel established, not tentative.
Small tree, big entrance. The kind of spring color that completely changes the mood of a corner.
  • peak to near-peak bloom
  • early leaf-out underway
  • strong flowering across the canopy
  • nice contrast with the Pugster butterfly bush behind it
April 13, 2026
Peach held inside the wider planting
▸
A day after peak, the peach is still the center of gravity of the brick-edged bed, but now the surrounding cast is starting to matter too. Pugster butterfly bush anchors the lower layer on one side, salvia and buddleia fill in the other, and the whole bed is beginning to read as a designed garden room rather than a single specimen dropped in place.
The peach is still the star, but the supporting cast is starting to take its places.
  • peach bloom still dominates the composition
  • Pugster, salvia, and buddleia defining the lower layer
  • bed geometry reads clearly with the brick edging
April 17, 2026
Leaf-out after bloom
▸
The dwarf peach has moved past the first rush of bloom and into a stronger spring push, with fresh green growth now taking over most of the canopy while a few deeper pink blossoms still hang on. It feels less like a flowering stick now and more like a young tree settling into its framework.
The flowers made the entrance, but the leaves are what make it start looking like a tree.
  • leaf-out is now doing most of the visual work
  • a handful of pink blossoms still hanging on
  • young branch structure easier to read than at peak bloom
April 21, 2026
Leaf clusters taking over
▸
The dwarf peach has clearly crossed into its leaf stage now, with clusters of long narrow foliage filling out each branch tip and very little of the earlier flower moment left. It looks healthy and orderly here, more settled than flashy.
The blossoms were the announcement. This is the part where the tree gets down to work.
  • leaf clusters now dominating every branch tip
  • flower phase has mostly passed
  • tree looks healthy, evenly leafed, structurally clean
April 27, 2026
Settled into green
▸
Fully in its post-bloom leaf stage now, with dense green clusters rounding out the branch tips and giving the small tree a much fuller canopy. The brick-edged planting pocket makes the trunk and shape easy to read, while the surrounding perennials are beginning to fill in around it without stealing the scene.
The spring show is over, and the little tree looks ready to spend the season growing.
  • fresh green leaf clusters filling the canopy evenly
  • tree form clear above the brick-edged planting pocket
  • nearby sedum and perennial foliage filling the lower layers
March 29, 2026
Before the show
▸
A quieter look at the dwarf peach before the pink show started, bare branching still exposed, buds swelling along the stems, and just enough movement to hint that bloom was close. This is the useful stage to remember later, because once the tree explodes into flower it's easy to forget how quickly it turned.
All structure and promise, the kind of in-between moment that only feels dramatic after you've seen what comes next.
  • buds swelling along the branches
  • tree still mostly bare
  • pre-bloom structure easy to see
