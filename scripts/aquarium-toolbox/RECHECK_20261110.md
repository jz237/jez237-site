## Reproduced failures

- A salt target entered as SG `1.000754` (1 ppt) calculated successfully but was
  omitted from storage. Its shared link reopened at the example target instead.
  Calculation, persistence and link validation disagreed about conversion noise.
- Exact SI endpoints converted to inches could invalidate an otherwise valid
  backup. Saving raw conversion noise could reset unrelated settings on reload.
- With a pipe bore of 1,000 mm, switch to US and press the slider's End key:
  rounding the already-clamped endpoint produced a value above the domain limit.
  Small gallon endpoints had the corresponding lower-limit problem.
- A link containing only `{v:100,dose:5}` produced a dose using an invented label
  water volume. Missing measurements must not be replaced silently with examples.
- Rename a tank with volume `151.41647136` L: it became `151.416471` L because a
  name-only save reconverted the rounded volume shown in the dialog.
- Repeated changes could lose the starting reading (and every zero-change result)
  when a small reading was subtracted from a much larger new-water reading.
  Full nitrate changes and pump-curve endpoints had related cancellation cases.
- The RO advice blamed cold water for reduced salt rejection. DuPont FilmTec's
  *Factors Affecting RO Membrane Performance*, June 2025, page 2, states that
  **increased** temperature increases salt passage; cold water reduces output.
  Keep production and rejection separate, check pressure and stable readings.

## Repair contracts

`normalizeInputs` is the common field/type/range contract for calculating, storing,
restoring and sharing. Only arithmetic-scale noise at nonzero limits is normalized;
missing numbers, non-finite values and real out-of-range values still fail. Explicit
legacy migrations remain, but no generic fill-with-defaults is allowed in a data link.
A broken link shows incomplete fields and no result until the visitor supplies the
measurements or deliberately chooses Reset. Imports remain atomic on failure.

Sliders preserve exact endpoints; round an interior editing increment before the
final clamp. Never change typed quantities to fit a convenience slider window.
Renaming a saved tank preserves its canonical volume, while a real volume edit or
size shortcut still changes it. Unit switches remain presentation-only.

Use positive weighted blends for repeated dilution and pump interpolation. Nitrate's
post-change equilibrium is computed directly rather than subtracting two large terms.
These are algebraically equivalent formulas with exact zero/full-change endpoints.
