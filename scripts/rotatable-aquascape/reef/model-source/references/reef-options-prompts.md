# Reef option fish: reference provenance

Generated October 19, 2026 through fal with `openai/gpt-image-2` (quality high), at
the user's request for the highest quality. 44 paid generations for six species (four remade the coral beauty, five the watchman goby, one the foxface's paired fins);
`fal-requests.json` records every request id, including four rejected plates.
These are generated illustrations, not photographs of measured specimens.

Per species: an accepted lateral plate, a masked clean-flank edit, a second
masked pass for remaining fin traces, and dorsal and front views made as edits
of the lateral plate (so markings and proportions match). `prepare_plates.py`
composites and pads the textures; `trace_profiles.py` reads
`trace-specs.json` (hand-read landmarks) and writes `profiles.json`.

Natural history: Wikipedia articles on each species (descriptions, habitat and
behavior), cross-checked against the prompts and the behavior notes in
`../../MarineBehavior.ts`.
