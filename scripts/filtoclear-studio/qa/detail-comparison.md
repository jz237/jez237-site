The built-in GPT image tool generated `gpt-detail-reference-v2.png` using the official OASE exterior photo and manual overview. The complete prompt is in `gpt-detail-prompt.txt`. ## Comparison and changes

- Initial barrel was smooth and too blue-gray. The generated reference and OASE photo show charcoal molded plastic and fine vertical rib panels. Added narrow merged ribs, adjusted dark plastic grain/finish, and preserved the removable front cutaway. The rib panels use just two additional meshes.
- Initial clamping band appeared silver and its latch was simplified. The exterior photo shows a dark band. Added a coated-metal finish, rolled edges, hinge pins and latch detail.
- Initial handle formed a tall tubular arch. Both references show a low rear handle. Replaced it with a flat molded grip around the rear of the UVC cover, with grip ridges and pivots. Existing cleaning lift and rod behavior are retained.
- Initial UVC head was a plain rounded box. Added longitudinal grooves that curve over its front, a cover seam, recessed blue clip, inset top panel and rear cable gland.
- Initial hose tails were straight cylinders with bright rings. Added stepped hollow profiles, finer ridges, larger union grips, seals and lid sockets, and reduced the opacity of clear fittings.
- Adjusted the fill-light angle and warmth to reveal molding against the dark plastic without changing the overall studio layout.

Unchanged: 32 educational assemblies; foam order; explosion offsets; guided lessons; hydraulic behavior; labels and part identifiers. No generated internal plumbing or extra functional components were added.
