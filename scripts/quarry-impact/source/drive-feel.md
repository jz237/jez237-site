# Water, fire and vehicle weight — September 30, 2026

This pass addresses puddles without tire reactions, predictable fires, a cramped derby and excessively soft bodywork. Original photographic assets, all 38 ElevenLabs recordings and all 23 deployed multiplayer authority inputs are retained.

The solo derby grows from 92 to 128 metres in diameter, a 93.6% area increase. Its offset center at (18,14) keeps the existing workshop and heavy quarry machinery outside the barrier. Ninety-six physical barriers, a larger fence, terrain-conforming ground, extended photographic gravel deposits, 43-metre starting positions, AI targeting bounds, the minimap and overhead camera use this layout. Mode-specific ramps are excluded from static batching so hiding their graphics also removes their collision. Racing and playground restore the original perimeter and ramps. Online authority retains the deployed layout.

Water contact uses the twelve original irregular puddle contours and their actual height and axis compression. Each grounded tire produces outward ballistic spray and expanding ripples according to speed and shoreline depth. Stationary, airborne and dry contacts emit nothing; puddles suppress dry gravel dust. Ripples clip to the water outline. Pools are capped at 768 drops and 48 ripples, with reduced emission on High and Medium. Progression uses fixed 60 Hz steps; pause, inspection and reset are covered by tests. No additional large models or texture downloads are introduced.

Car masses remain 1,480 kg for the coupe, 1,650 for the sedan and 1,290 for the hatch. The old half-metre-high collision proxy gave the bodies unrealistically small roll inertia. Explicit body inertia now uses a 1.3-metre height and a modestly lower center of mass, with reduced restitution and slightly higher angular damping. Collision damage requires closing speed along the contact normal: sustained low-speed pushing has physical resistance but no repeated health loss. A maximum 23-point damaging impact is survivable. Metal audio severity follows physical impulse separately from health loss.

Body deformation receives a softer early-impact response, with smaller localized dents and slower attachment wear. Heavy, repeated hits still buckle outer and inner panels, fracture glazing, loosen hinges, displace wheels and release connected plastic bumpers and other assemblies. Health-driven engine, steering and suspension consequences remain; repair restores the authored geometry and clears all damage zones. This is rigid-body driving with visual deformation, not structural soft-body simulation.

Fire requires health at or below 18% and substantial front or rear damage. Each qualifying damage episode gets one 24% ignition assessment, rather than a per-frame lottery. Eligible cars wait 3–11 seconds before igniting. Individual fires have 30–70 seconds of fuel and fade out, leaving persistent soot until repair. Only 12% of eligible fires can produce one delayed fuel burst, with additional requirements for sustained heat and health at or below 6%; thus explosions remain a small minority of critical wrecks. Crushed doors, roofs or low health alone cannot ignite. Less severe engine damage may release pale coolant steam.

One dominant engine-bay or rear-leak source is chosen from physical damage and each car's varied profile. Short curling flame tongues follow that source as the car moves. Smoke remains in world space, with coherent three-dimensional billow noise, buoyancy, wind and sunlight response. Size, opacity, soot and flicker vary; the two-light and 640-puff budgets remain. Existing offline-produced ElevenLabs recordings provide spatial flame, crackle and burst audio. No audio generation, credential use or additional spending occurred.

The debug-only `seedFireStress` fixture deliberately selects eight ignitable non-explosive states and extends their fuel for a sustained maximum-effects benchmark. Gameplay never invokes it. Natural ignition probabilities, finite fuel, frame-rate consistency, reset and source placement are separately tested. The stress fixture does not represent ordinary derby fire frequency.

Reproduction: `npm run build`; run all `tests/*.test.ts` except `network*.test.ts` for the authorized solo suite. `QUARRY_DRIVE_OUTPUT=<fresh-directory> node tools/drive-feel-qa.mjs` checks actual wet wheels, two speeds, varied fires, delayed bursts, repair and sound controls at 1440p. `QUARRY_BROWSER_QA_OUTPUT=<fresh-directory> node tools/browser-qa.mjs` completes the playable modes. `QUARRY_PERF_OUTPUT=<fresh-directory> QUARRY_PERF_SECONDS=60 node tools/performance-pass-qa.mjs` measures real-time derby and maximum-effects rendering. `source/drive-feel-revision.json` and compressed original sources keep the preceding release recoverable without replacing older frozen baselines.
