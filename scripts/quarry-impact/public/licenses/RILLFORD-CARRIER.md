# Rillford Carrier

Original Quarry Impact panel van artwork, authored in `src/van-asset.ts`. Its closed cargo shell, rear barn doors, cab, inline-four engine bay and interior use no donor meshes, external textures, manufacturer marks or reference-game assets. The van shares Quarry Impact's original Rook stamped wheel tooling at its own tyre size.

Gameplay uses the shared suspension, damage, paint, glass and audio systems. The van reuses the existing four-cylinder audio bank at a lower pitch; it has no separately recorded engine soundtrack. The vehicle artwork remains beta.
