# Tern 1400

Original Quarry Impact vehicle artwork, authored in 2026. No third-party vehicle model, marque, badge or texture is incorporated.

Bodywork, glazing, transverse engine, interior and trim are generated by src/tern-asset.ts. The original Rook wheel tooling is reused without changing the Rook asset. Existing bundled four-cylinder sound is reused with a 1.04 pitch multiplier; it is not a dedicated recording.

Vehicle artwork, severe-crash details and sound remain beta.
