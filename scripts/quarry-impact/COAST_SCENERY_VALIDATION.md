# Coastal and forest scenery

Seabrook Coast Circuit and Hazelwood Forest Rally now use the shared scenic dressing system. Seabrook has an open northern sea horizon with world-space ripple/shore colour, rocky headland, banded round lighthouse with lantern and balcony, photographic trees, service paddock and spectator shelters. Hazelwood has denser photographic pine/spruce/maple foliage, a lodge roof/chimney, stacked cut timber, spectators, service vehicle and wooded hills. Original parody venue signs identify both courses.

The previous box trees and bare sheds are removed. Distant foliage follows surrounding landscape height. Decorative placements remain behind existing barriers; terrain, road surface queries, solids, checkpoint samples, physics, driving controls and demo camera logic are unchanged. The previous four themes preserve their placement logic. Assets reuse existing licensed game photographs; no new download or asset licence dependency. Near trees remain photographic cards and several structures remain simple meshes: this is a playable scenery improvement, not Wreckfest 2 visual parity.
