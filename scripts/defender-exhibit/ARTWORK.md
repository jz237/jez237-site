# Williams artwork sources

Joust and Robotron printed designs replace the simplified procedural approximations. Source files are retained in public/joust and public/robotron. Texture canvases crop the horizontal panel and front apron separately. Joysticks and buttons align with the scan openings. Cabinet dimensions and artwork fit remain approximate; scan colors and production variants are not independently calibrated.

- Joust panel: https://arcarc.xmission.com/Arcade%20by%20Title/Joust/joust_overlay_600dpi.JPG — original upright yellow/blue design, wings, birds, flames and Williams apron.
- Joust side: https://classicgaming.cc/classics/joust/art/joust_sideart.pdf via https://classicgaming.cc/classics/joust/graphics — archived vector artwork converted to SVG using PyMuPDF. The brown-background design is used; the alternate blue-rider colorway is excluded by the SVG viewport.
- Joust reference: https://www.coinopstuff.com/items/1982-williams-joust-upright-arcade-machine-4465/
- Robotron panel: https://arcarc.xmission.com/Arcade%20by%20Title/Robotron/robotron%20cpo%20600dpi.jpg — original upright panel scan, including central targeting illustration and angular 2084 apron.
- Robotron side: https://www.ebay.com/itm/225059765128 — StickerSmith reproduction reference, not a factory scan. Image: https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/2xgAAOSwnSNiwzxi/s-l1200.jpg . Separate left/right designs preserve readable vertical 2084 numerals and red/blue stripes.

Williams attribution is retained. Marquees remain the previously sourced originals. Bezel geometry remains plain and is not claimed as an original printed bezel scan.
