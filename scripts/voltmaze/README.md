## Difficulty

One table: `RAMP` in `js/config.js` (per sector, `[first room, last room before the lift]`, interpolated per room; `rooms` = how many doors the lift is from the arrival lift). Keys: count (fractional: the sim rolls the fraction per room, and the reinforcements likewise, so a sector climbs without whole-robot steps), shoot (robots aiming/firing at once), fire, boltV, lead, aim, warn (every telegraph), boot, first (delay after powering up before a robot may aim), burst (bolts per shot, fanned `BURST_A` apart and shown by the glint; marksmen fire one), reinf (reinforcements that warp in while a room is live, see `REINF`), spd, smart (chance a robot checks for walls; the rest can be lured), walls, seek / seekV (the Seeker), drops, bossHP / bossRate. Bosses: `BOSSES` (attack list per phase, `mod` = the boss's own numbers for a shared attack) + `ATTACK` (tell in units of `warn`, act seconds; volleys and sprays lock on for the last 30 % of their tell). Every tell lasts at least the RAMP `warn` (sim `beginAttack`, robot aim glints, ripper crouches, mortar rings; ramp_check audits it).

`DIFFICULTY` + `applyDiff()` scale it: multipliers, shifts, `burst` / `reinf` scaling and `fade` (pulls a level back toward NORMAL sector by sector: EASY is the gentle start in sector 1 and NORMAL's numbers by the Reactor, with an extra life; HARD fades 12 %). Warnings never below `MIN_WARN` 0.45 s. Score multipliers 0.6 / 1 / 1.5 / 2.2. Endless walks the same curve via `endlessLevel(depth)` (`ENDLESS`: full strength after ~66 rooms; the free upgrade per floor comes in the order weapon, dash, EMP, plate, jammer). Shop salvage = sector points / `SALVAGE` (25), so a typical run owns about `TYPICAL_UP`. The attract demo starts two rooms short of the lift.

## Look and sound (stage 2)

- Five looks in `LOOKS` (js/render.js): floor set, light rig, fog, haze, image-light level (`envI`, scales `scene.environmentIntensity`: three ignores a material's `envMapIntensity` when it uses `scene.environment`), shafts, and `depth`: from sector 2 on the room is a platform over the sector's depths (skirt with lit rim and ribs, bridges with rails at the doors, structure and features far below, layered mist planes that darken whatever is deeper).
- Wet floor: `SPILL_FRAG` draws one instanced quad per field / seal / door lamp / overhead pool: glow spill plus a streaky mirror image toward the camera where the floor's roughness map says it is wet.
- ACTION camera (option CAMERA: ACTION / WHOLE ROOM): frames the suit and every live threat, never tighter than ~60 % of the room, widens fast and closes in slowly; the lift ride has its own close camera.
- Effects: EMP dome (`DOME_FRAG`), lightning strikes, per-style hazard bursts (`zoneFx`, from the sim's `zone` event), boss quakes, Seeker inbound telegraph (entry seal pulses + winding ring), the entry seal rising behind you, class-coloured rings under every robot. All warmed up at load; 44 programs, constant through a full playthrough.
- Audio: `js/audio.js` SFX list + `ALIAS` (uiback, boltwall, drop, life, pwend reuse clips at another pitch), `VOICE` (12 lines), music per sector + boss + title + stings. One `say()` gate in main.js (key lines always, colour lines >= 26 s apart), NARRATION option.
