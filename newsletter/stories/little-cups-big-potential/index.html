Freshwater / Plant-room notes

Little cups, big planted-tank potential

A closer look at tissue-cultured aquarium plants, from sealed cup to a new underwater landscape.

Source published: Evergreen guide · no publication date shown
Selected for this preview: November 1, 2026

Tropica describes its 1-2-Grow plants as tissue-cultured plants raised in laboratory conditions. The company says they are supplied without snails, algae or pesticides and can be used in aquariums containing shrimp and fish.

The same guide cautions that these young plants need attention to their growing conditions from the start. It also distinguishes them from other product types, including mature potted plants and plants attached to wood or stone.

What it means for a fishkeeper

These are the manufacturer's claims about its own product range, not an independent test of every tissue-culture brand. Check the individual plant's care requirements before buying.
