The patient science behind captive-bred fish

Meet the research that helps more ornamental fish complete their life cycles in managed care.

Source published: February 25, 2025
Selected for this preview: November 1, 2026

University of Florida IFAS describes research helping fish farmers breed popular marine species, including clownfish and blue tangs. The aim is to support aquaculture while reducing the need to collect fish from wild reefs.

The article connects this work with conservation and the aquarium trade. Progress with one species does not mean that every fish can be bred the same way, or that every specimen offered for sale is captive-bred.

What it means for a fishkeeper

Ask about the origin of an individual fish rather than assuming its breeding history from its appearance. This background feature does not represent Hidden Reef's current stock.
