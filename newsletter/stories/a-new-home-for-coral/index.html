A second home for irreplaceable corals

A second ocean-based gene bank gives Florida's coral diversity another place to survive.

Source published: August 20, 2026
Selected for this preview: November 1, 2026

Coral Restoration Foundation reported moving approximately 1,000 corals representing 130 genotypes to its Elbow Nursery. This was the first stage of a planned transfer of about 3,500 corals, intended to replicate the genetic diversity held at its Tavernier Nursery.

The goal is to avoid keeping irreplaceable genetic stock at a single ocean-based site. The foundation says the two in-water gene banks also complement land-based banking at The Reef Institute. Different nursery conditions offer options, not a guarantee against heat stress.

What it means for a fishkeeper

For reef keepers, this is a window into the work behind coral propagation and conservation. It is not a product announcement or a claim that these corals are available for home aquariums.
