A greener aquarium starts with the right light

Before chasing brighter lights, match your plants to the aquarium you actually want to maintain.

Source published: Evergreen guide · no publication date shown
Selected for this preview: November 1, 2026

Tropica groups aquarium plants by their requirements, including how much light they need. Its guidance emphasizes matching the lighting to the plants rather than treating a brighter fixture as an automatic upgrade.

The guide also notes that tank depth, fixture efficiency and reflectors affect how much light reaches the plants. A number printed on a light is only part of the picture.

What it means for a fishkeeper

Start with the plants you want to grow and check their individual requirements. Use the manufacturer's guide as a starting point, not a universal lighting prescription for every tank.
